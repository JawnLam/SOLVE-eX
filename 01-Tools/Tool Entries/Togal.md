# Tögal (Direct Crossing)

**One-line summary:** Dzogchen's second major practice — uses specific posture, gaze, and breath to evoke direct visionary experience of luminosity, following trekchö stabilization.

**When to reach for it:** After trekchö has stabilized recognition of awareness's nature, within formal Nyingma Dzogchen transmission, when teacher determines the practitioner is ready for visionary practice.

## Purpose

Tögal ('leapover' or 'direct crossing') is the second main practice of Dzogchen, building on stabilized trekchö. It uses specific physical posture, gaze direction (sky, dark room, sun reflected on water — never the sun directly), and breath to evoke direct visionary experience of luminosity. The visions are not products of imagination — they are reported as spontaneous manifestation of awareness's inherent luminosity, structured according to traditional descriptions. Considered the most secret of Tibetan practices. Outsiders typically cannot access without formal lineage initiation; descriptions in print are deliberately incomplete.

## How To Use

Strictly within Nyingma Dzogchen lineage transmission, after trekchö stabilization, under personal guidance of a qualified teacher. The practice cannot be self-administered safely or effectively. Outside lineage, study the philosophical context (the doctrine that awareness has spontaneous luminous display) rather than the technique. The technique itself is transmitted privately, in extended retreat conditions, with detailed teacher-supervision because the visionary experiences require lineage-trained interpretation and stabilization. Most students of Dzogchen never receive tögal instruction even after long practice.
