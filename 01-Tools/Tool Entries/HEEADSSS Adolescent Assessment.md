# HEEADSSS Adolescent Assessment

**One-line summary:** Mnemonic interview protocol for adolescent patients — Home, Education/Employment, Eating, Activities, Drugs, Sexuality, Suicide/depression, Safety.

**When to reach for it:** Conducting clinical assessment of an adolescent patient where developmental-stage-relevant risk factors must be surfaced and a generic adult-focused interview would miss critical domains.

## Purpose

HEEADSSS is a mnemonic interview protocol developed for clinical assessment of adolescent patients (Cohen 1991, expanded by Goldenring & Rosen 2004). The letters: (H)ome — family structure, conflict, safety; (E)ducation/Employment — school, work, performance; (E)ating — patterns, body image; (A)ctivities — peer group, interests, screen time; (D)rugs — substance use including alcohol, tobacco, others; (S)exuality — orientation, activity, consent, contraception; (S)uicide/depression — mood, ideation, plan, attempt; (S)afety — violence, weapons, internet safety, riding with impaired drivers. Each domain is opened with normalizing language and explored. Adapts the medical interview to developmental-stage-relevant domains.

## How To Use

Begin with developmentally-appropriate framing: 'I ask all my patients your age the same questions to make sure I'm taking good care of them. Some are personal — let me know if you'd rather not answer something.' Then work through the letters in approximate order, generally moving from less sensitive (Home) to more sensitive (Sexuality, Suicide, Safety). Use normalizing introduction for each domain ('Lots of kids your age have started thinking about...'). Listen for what's underneath — adolescents often raise concerns indirectly. Have parent/guardian leave room for sensitive sections per clinic policy. Adjust frequency of full HEEADSSS by visit type — annual full screen, problem visits can focus on relevant letters.
