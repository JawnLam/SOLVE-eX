# Johari Window

**One-line summary:** A 2x2 mapping known/unknown to self × known/unknown to others — Open Arena, Blind Spot, Hidden, Unknown — to expand self-awareness and group dialogue.

**When to reach for it:** Team-building workshops, feedback conversations, coaching sessions; making implicit interpersonal dynamics explicit.

## Purpose

The Window names four regions of self-knowledge: what you and others both see (Arena), what others see but you don't (Blind Spot), what you see but hide (Façade), and what neither sees (Unknown). Different intentional moves expand each region: feedback shrinks Blind Spot; disclosure shrinks Façade; collaborative exploration probes Unknown. The tool gives team development a structured language.

## How To Use

1) Map yourself on the four quadrants. 2) Identify which quadrant feels most consequential to grow. 3) Choose the expansion move: solicit feedback (Blind Spot), disclose (Façade), explore (Unknown). 4) Repeat with a partner. 5) Track how the boundaries shift over time.
