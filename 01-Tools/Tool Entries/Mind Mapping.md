# Mind Mapping

**One-line summary:** A radial diagram with a central concept and branching sub-concepts — Buzan's structured technique for note-taking and idea elaboration.

**When to reach for it:** Note-taking from lectures or books, brainstorming around a single topic, planning a project's components, studying for retention.

## Purpose

Linear note-taking imposes a sequential structure that may not match the structure of the underlying ideas. Mind Mapping uses a radial topology — central concept, branching sub-concepts, sub-sub-concepts — that mirrors associative thinking. Buzan's specific technique adds color, image, and curved branches to engage visual memory. The tool is the structured radial elaboration.

## How To Use

1) Write the central concept in the page center. 2) Branch out main sub-concepts. 3) Sub-branch each. 4) Use color, images, varied line width for emphasis. 5) Add cross-links between related branches. 6) Review periodically — the spatial structure aids recall.
