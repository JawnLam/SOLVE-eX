# Hype Cycle

**One-line summary:** A canonical curve of technology-adoption emotion — Innovation Trigger → Peak of Inflated Expectations → Trough of Disillusionment → Slope of Enlightenment → Plateau of Productivity.

**When to reach for it:** Technology-investment timing; managing organizational expectations during adoption; reading market narratives for over-hype or under-appreciation; presenting maturity assessments.

## Purpose

The Gartner curve captures the well-observed pattern that technology adoption is not monotone — initial enthusiasm overshoots reality, then a backlash undershoots reality, then sustainable adoption settles in. Mapping current technologies to positions on the curve helps avoid both peak-buying (overpaying for inflated expectations) and trough-skipping (abandoning during the disillusionment phase). The tool is the explicit calibration: 'where on the curve do I think we are?' before timing investments.

## How To Use

1) Identify the technology and its rough adoption-timeline position. 2) Estimate where on the curve it currently sits. 3) Plan accordingly: if at peak, wait; if at trough, evaluate; if on slope, invest. 4) Recognize that not all technologies traverse the full curve — some die in the trough. 5) Update annually.
