# Wind Tunneling (Scenario Stress-Testing)

**One-line summary:** Run an existing strategy through each scenario as if in a wind tunnel — assess robustness, identify vulnerabilities, find strategy modifications that improve robustness.

**When to reach for it:** After scenario construction; before committing to a strategy under uncertainty; periodic review of existing strategies as new scenarios emerge.

## Purpose

Scenarios are not predictions but stress-tests. Wind Tunneling is the structured stress-testing procedure: take the existing strategy, run it through each scenario, ask what happens. The exercise identifies vulnerabilities (strategy fails in scenario X), opportunities (strategy underperforms in scenario Y but a modification would capture it), and robustness (strategy succeeds across all scenarios). The output is either a more-robust modified strategy or explicit recognition of the bets the existing strategy implicitly makes.

## How To Use

1) Take the proposed strategy. 2) For each scenario, imagine the strategy unfolding. 3) Document outcomes: what works, what fails, what's exposed. 4) Identify vulnerabilities and opportunities. 5) Modify strategy to improve cross-scenario robustness. 6) Or accept the implicit bets and design contingencies for scenarios the strategy doesn't handle well.
