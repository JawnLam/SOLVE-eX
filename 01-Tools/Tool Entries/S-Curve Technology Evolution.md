# S-Curve / Technology Evolution Analysis

**One-line summary:** Technologies follow S-shaped adoption-or-performance curves: slow start, rapid improvement, maturity plateau — and a successor curve typically rises before the incumbent peaks.

**When to reach for it:** Technology-investment timing; identifying when current technology is reaching maturity and a successor is emerging; R&D portfolio rebalancing.

## Purpose

Foster and others observed that technology improvement under sustained investment follows an S-curve: initial slow gains, then rapid improvement once the architecture stabilizes, then diminishing returns as physical limits approach. Strategically: the largest mistakes are made at the inflection points — investing too long in mature tech, or abandoning emerging tech before its rapid-growth phase. The tool is the explicit positioning of current and successor technologies on their respective S-curves.

## How To Use

1) Identify the technology and its performance metric. 2) Plot historical performance against cumulative R&D investment or time. 3) Estimate position on the curve. 4) Identify candidate successor technologies. 5) Plot their early-S-curve positions. 6) Time transitions to ride successive curves.
