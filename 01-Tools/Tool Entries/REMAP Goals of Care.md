# REMAP Goals-of-Care Framework

**One-line summary:** Five-step protocol for shifting goals-of-care conversations from disease-focused to values-focused — Reframe, Expect emotion, Map values, Align, Propose plan.

**When to reach for it:** A patient with serious or advanced illness is at an inflection point where the question is no longer 'what's the next treatment?' but 'what are we trying to accomplish, given the values that matter?' — and the conversation needs structure to reach values before plan.

## Purpose

REMAP (Childers, Arnold, et al., 2017) is a five-step goals-of-care conversation protocol developed in oncology and palliative care. The letters: (R)eframe — acknowledge that the situation has changed and current approach may not be working; (E)xpect emotion — respond to the emotion that arises with empathic statements before moving on; (M)ap patient values — explore what matters to the patient: what they hope for, fear, want to avoid; (A)lign with values — reflect back the values heard; (P)ropose plan — based on aligned values, propose specific medical recommendations. Used to shift conversations from treatment-options-focused (which presupposes goals) to values-focused (which discovers goals first).

## How To Use

Before the conversation: review the clinical situation; have a tentative recommendation but don't lead with it. Begin: Reframe — 'We're at a point where we need to step back and think about what matters to you.' Allow emotion to arise; respond with NURSE statements (Name, Understand, Respect, Support, Explore) before moving on. Map: 'What worries you most? What gives you hope? What do you want to make sure happens, or doesn't happen?' Listen for values, not just preferences. Align: reflect back — 'It sounds like time with family matters most, and being aware of your surroundings is important.' Propose: 'Given that, here's what I'd recommend...' The protocol disciplines reaching values before plan.
