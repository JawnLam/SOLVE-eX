# Process Work (Mindell)

**One-line summary:** Arnold Mindell's Jungian + Tao + worldwork synthesis. Tracks "secondary processes" (signals that contradict the primary identity) across body, dream, relationship, and worldwork channels — using edge-of-awareness signals as cognitive data.

**When to reach for it:** Working with clients who present a single coherent self-narrative but show somatic, dream, or relational signals that contradict it. Also useful in group-conflict facilitation ("worldwork") where the surface conflict masks deeper structural dynamics.

## Purpose

Mindell builds on Jung's observation that what's rejected from the conscious identity emerges in dream, body, and relationship. Process Work makes this explicit and trackable: the client has a primary process (the conscious self-story) and a secondary process (what the body/dreams/relationships keep insisting). The therapist amplifies the secondary process until it becomes consciously known — the "edge" between the two is where transformation happens.

## How To Use

1. **Identify the primary process** — what's the client's conscious self-statement?
2. **Track secondary signals** — body tension, repeated dream images, recurring relational dynamics that contradict primary.
3. **Amplify** — bring the secondary signal into awareness via the channel it's expressed in (movement for body, voice for dreams, role-play for relational).
4. **Identify the edge** — the threshold where the client cannot yet hold both.
5. **Cross the edge** — explore what the secondary process is saying. Integration emerges as the client expands their self-definition.
