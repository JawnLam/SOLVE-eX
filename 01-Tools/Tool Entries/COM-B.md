# COM-B (Capability-Opportunity-Motivation-Behavior)

**One-line summary:** A behavior change model — Behavior = f(Capability, Opportunity, Motivation), where each has physical and psychological/social sub-components.

**When to reach for it:** Public health intervention design; clinical behavior-change; organizational behavior change; designing nudges that target the right deficit.

## Purpose

COM-B is the academic-behavioral-science model behind the Behavior Change Wheel. It decomposes behavior into three necessary conditions: capability (physical and psychological — can the person do it?), opportunity (physical and social — does the context support it?), and motivation (reflective and automatic — does the person want to do it?). Each must be sufficient for the behavior to occur. The diagnostic is identifying which condition is the binding constraint and designing interventions accordingly.

## How To Use

1) Define the target behavior precisely. 2) Assess Capability: physical (skills, fitness) and psychological (knowledge, decision-making). 3) Assess Opportunity: physical (resources, time) and social (norms, support). 4) Assess Motivation: reflective (beliefs, intentions) and automatic (habits, emotions). 5) Identify the binding deficit. 6) Design interventions targeting it.
