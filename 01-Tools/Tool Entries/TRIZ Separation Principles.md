# Separation Principles (TRIZ contradiction resolution)

**One-line summary:** Resolve physical contradictions (where a single parameter must take opposite values) by separating the parameter in time, space, scale, or condition.

**When to reach for it:** Physical contradictions where the same component must simultaneously have opposite properties (be rigid AND flexible, be hot AND cold, be present AND absent).

## Purpose

Technical contradictions (improving X degrades Y) are addressed by the 40 Principles. Physical contradictions (X must be both A and not-A simultaneously) are harder — they appear impossible. Separation Principles resolve them by recognizing that the requirement is rarely truly simultaneous — usually the requirement varies across time (rigid when locked, flexible when adjusting), space (hot at the surface, cold inside), scale (porous at the molecular level, sealed at the macro level), or condition (rigid when full, flexible when empty).

## How To Use

1) State the physical contradiction precisely (X must be both A and not-A). 2) Ask: time separation — can it be A at some times and not-A at others? 3) Space separation — can different regions have different properties? 4) Scale separation — micro vs. macro? 5) Condition separation — depends on context? 6) Choose the most promising separation; design accordingly.
