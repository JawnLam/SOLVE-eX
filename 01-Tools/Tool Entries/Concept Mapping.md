# Concept Mapping

**One-line summary:** A hierarchical diagram of concepts connected by labeled relationship-arrows — Novak's structured technique for representing conceptual understanding.

**When to reach for it:** Studying complex domains, assessing student understanding, surfacing implicit conceptual structure, organizing literature review findings.

## Purpose

Concept Maps differ from Mind Maps in two ways: structure is hierarchical (general concepts on top, specific below) rather than radial, and the connecting arrows are labeled with the type of relationship ('causes,' 'is a part of,' 'requires'). The result is a richer representation that captures not just associations but the structure of the conceptual relationships.

## How To Use

1) List the concepts. 2) Rank from general to specific. 3) Place general concepts at the top. 4) Connect related concepts with arrows. 5) Label each arrow with the relationship type. 6) Add cross-links where concepts in different branches relate. 7) Refine through iteration.
