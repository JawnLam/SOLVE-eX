# GTD (Getting Things Done — Allen)

**One-line summary:** A complete personal productivity system — Capture, Clarify, Organize, Reflect, Engage — that externalizes commitments to free cognitive resources.

**When to reach for it:** Knowledge workers managing many competing commitments; reducing the cognitive cost of remembering open loops; restoring focus when mental clutter dominates.

## Purpose

Allen's central observation: the mind is poor at storing and reminding but excellent at processing. GTD externalizes the storage and reminding into a trusted system, freeing the mind for processing. The five-stage workflow (capture everything, clarify what each item means, organize by context and time, reflect periodically, engage with the right item now) creates a complete system that, when followed, produces a 'mind like water' state — responsive and uncluttered. The method has been transformative for many knowledge workers.

## How To Use

1) Capture every commitment, task, idea into a trusted inbox. 2) Clarify: for each item, decide what it is (actionable? trash? reference?). 3) Organize: actionable items into next-action lists by context. 4) Reflect: weekly review to keep the system trustworthy. 5) Engage: choose the right next action based on context, time, energy, priority.
