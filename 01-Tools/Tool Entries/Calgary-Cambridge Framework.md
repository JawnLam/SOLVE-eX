# Calgary-Cambridge Framework

**One-line summary:** A 71-skill medical-consultation framework integrating content and process across five phases — initiating the session, gathering information, physical examination, explanation and planning, closing.

**When to reach for it:** Teaching or assessing clinical communication, or mapping a clinician's consultation behavior against an evidence-based skills inventory. Embedded in many UK/Commonwealth medical schools' OSCE assessment.

## Purpose

Integrates content (the medical history) with process (how the clinician asks, listens, explains). Each phase has explicit skills — opening with rapport, signposting transitions, open-then-closed questioning, teach-back, negotiating a plan jointly. Distinct from SOAP (documentation-focused) in being a *real-time* dialogic scaffold.

## How To Use

1. **Initiating** — Establish rapport, identify reason for visit, negotiate agenda.
2. **Gathering Information** — Patient narrative + clinician framework (background, ICE: Ideas/Concerns/Expectations).
3. **Physical Examination** — Performed with explanation throughout.
4. **Explanation and Planning** — Provide diagnostic info, check understanding, develop shared plan.
5. **Closing** — Summarize, agree next steps, safety-netting.

Cross-cutting: building the relationship, providing structure.
