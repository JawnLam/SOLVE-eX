# Nyasa (Mantra-Body Placement)

**One-line summary:** Tantric practice of mentally placing mantric energies on specific parts of the physical and subtle body — touching while reciting and visualizing.

**When to reach for it:** Preparing for extended yantra worship or sustained meditation where the body itself must be consecrated as the seat of practice, and where mere mental approach feels insufficient.

## Purpose

Nyasa ('placement') is a family of tantric practices in which the practitioner mentally places mantric energies on specific locations of the physical or subtle body. Each placement involves touching the location (often with mudra), reciting an associated mantra, and visualizing the presiding shakti. The cumulative effect: the body becomes consecrated as the seat of practice; awareness becomes simultaneously somatic, vocal, and symbolic. Variants include matrika nyasa (placing each of the 50 Sanskrit letters at different body points), kara nyasa (hand-placement), anga nyasa (limb-placement), and rishi nyasa (placing the rishi, meter, and presiding deity of a mantra).

## How To Use

Learn a specific nyasa sequence from a teacher (the full set of associations cannot be improvised). For each placement: touch the body location with the prescribed fingers/mudra, recite the mantra clearly, visualize the corresponding shakti or letter as a luminous form at that location. Move through the sequence in order. The full body is gradually consecrated. Used as a 5-15 minute prelude to a longer practice. Deepens with repetition; over time, the body remembers and arrives in the consecrated state more quickly.
