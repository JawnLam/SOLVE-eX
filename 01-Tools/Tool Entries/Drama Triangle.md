# Drama Triangle

**One-line summary:** Recurring conflict often patterns into three roles — Victim, Persecutor, Rescuer — that rotate among participants and sustain the dynamic.

**When to reach for it:** Recurring interpersonal conflicts, family-system patterns, workplace conflict diagnosis, coaching around stuck relationship dynamics.

## Purpose

Karpman observed that dysfunctional relationship patterns frequently organize into three complementary roles that rotate among participants. Recognizing the triangle in operation enables exit moves: the Victim claims power, the Persecutor stops attacking, the Rescuer stops rescuing. The tool names the structure so participants can step out of their role without escalating.

## How To Use

1) Identify the three positions in the current dynamic. 2) Notice your default role. 3) Notice how the roles rotate. 4) Choose a counter-move: the antidote to Victim is owning power; to Persecutor is owning vulnerability; to Rescuer is owning trust in the other's capacity. 5) Practice the exit without expecting the others to follow immediately.
