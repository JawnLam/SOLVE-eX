# IFS Self-Energy Cultivation

**One-line summary:** Cultivate access to the '8 Cs of Self' — calm, curiosity, compassion, confidence, clarity, courage, creativity, connectedness — as the leadership posture for inner work.

**When to reach for it:** When parts are dominant and Self feels absent; as a recurring practice between sessions; teaching IFS clients to access Self without the therapist's presence.

## Purpose

In IFS, the work depends on having Self present to relate to parts. The 8 Cs are the qualities of Self-energy; cultivating them is the practitioner's preparation for parts work. The cultivation is not a technique but a recurring stance — through breath, somatic noticing, or contemplative practice, the practitioner accesses each C and notices its presence or absence. With practice, Self becomes the default ground from which parts are encountered.

## How To Use

1) Before parts work, sit quietly. 2) Cycle through the 8 Cs: notice the felt sense of each. 3) Where a C is absent, notice without forcing. 4) Allow Self-energy to come forward. 5) Begin parts work from this ground. 6) Return to the 8 Cs whenever Self has receded.
