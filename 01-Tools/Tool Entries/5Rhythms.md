# 5Rhythms

**One-line summary:** Gabrielle Roth's movement-meditation practice traversing five rhythmic states — Flowing, Staccato, Chaos, Lyrical, Stillness. Movement as inquiry, not choreography.

**When to reach for it:** Anyone seeking embodied access to inner states or emotional integration through movement. Used in dance-therapy, addiction recovery, leadership work, and contemplative practice. Open-floor practice taught worldwide.

## Purpose

Roth observed that human movement passes through five recurring qualities — fluid, percussive, abandoned, expansive, still — and that each qualifies different emotional and cognitive territory. The Wave traverses all five in sequence, taking the mover through a complete emotional-somatic arc. The practice cultivates somatic literacy: the ability to recognize and inhabit different inner states through movement rather than through thought.

## How To Use

1. **Open with awareness** — Stand, feel ground, notice body. No agenda.
2. **Flowing** — Continuous, fluid, grounded movement. Receptive quality.
3. **Staccato** — Defined, percussive, edge-finding movement. Yang quality.
4. **Chaos** — Surrender control; allow the body to move beyond habitual patterns.
5. **Lyrical** — Light, free, expansive; integration after release.
6. **Stillness** — Movement becomes very slow, then ceases; rest in awareness.

Music is curated to support each rhythm; teacher holds space without choreographing. "Sweat your prayers."
