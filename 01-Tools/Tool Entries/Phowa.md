# Phowa (Consciousness Transfer)

**One-line summary:** Tibetan practice of 'transferring consciousness' — visualizing consciousness moving upward through the central channel out the crown chakra into the buddha-realm.

**When to reach for it:** Preparing for one's own death with intentional practice, or working with consciousness-direction visualization as a standalone training of subtle-body attention.

## Purpose

Phowa is most associated with preparation for death — the practitioner trains in directing consciousness at the moment of death so that it leaves through the crown chakra into a buddha-realm rather than through other openings into less favorable rebirths. As standalone visualization practice (not just death preparation), it trains the subtle-body attention: imagining the central channel, the crown opening, the upward movement of awareness. Multiple variants exist: Six Yogas of Naropa, Heart Essence of Longchenpa, Nyingma terma cycles. Distinct from concentration practice in that it has a directional somatic-imaginative target.

## How To Use

Within formal Tibetan Buddhist lineage transmission: receive empowerment and detailed instruction. The full practice involves preliminary refuge, visualization of a buddha-figure (often Amitabha) above the crown, visualization of the central channel running through the body, syllable visualizations at heart and crown, repeated upward-movement of awareness through the channel, and merging of consciousness with the buddha-figure. The practice is done in extended sessions over weeks; signs of accomplishment (sometimes a small opening at the crown that can be probed with a kusha-grass straw) are traditionally noted by the teacher. Outside lineage transmission, study as concept rather than attempt the visualization.
