# Subgame Perfect Equilibrium

**One-line summary:** An equilibrium of an extensive-form game that constitutes a Nash equilibrium of every subgame — rules out non-credible threats.

**When to reach for it:** Sequential games where Nash equilibrium identifies multiple solutions and the refinement matters; analyzing commitment problems and credible threats.

## Purpose

Nash equilibrium in extensive-form games can include non-credible threats — strategies that wouldn't be played if the relevant subgame were reached. Subgame Perfect Equilibrium refines Nash by requiring rationality at every subgame, ruling out non-credible threats and commitments. Backward induction in finite games produces SPE directly. The concept clarifies which equilibria are robust to off-equilibrium play.

## How To Use

1) Map the extensive form. 2) Find every subgame. 3) For each subgame, find its Nash equilibria. 4) The strategy profile is SPE if it is Nash on every subgame. 5) In finite games, backward induction yields the unique SPE.
