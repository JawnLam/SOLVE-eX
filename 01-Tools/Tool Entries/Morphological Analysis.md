# Morphological Analysis (Zwicky Box)

**One-line summary:** Enumerate the parameter space of a problem as an n-dimensional matrix of attribute combinations; explore all coherent configurations.

**When to reach for it:** Wicked problems where the parameter space is large and not pre-structured; technology forecasting; design-option enumeration; product-line architecture.

## Purpose

Zwicky's method makes the full possibility-space of a problem explicit by enumerating attributes as dimensions and values within each. The combinatorial expansion can be enormous, but the structured framing prevents the typical failure of analogical reasoning that visits only nearby configurations. The discipline is to enumerate fully, then prune by consistency and credibility, rather than to enumerate selectively from the start.

## How To Use

1) Identify the dimensions of the problem (5-12 typical). 2) For each dimension, list possible values. 3) The cross-product is the possibility space. 4) Filter by internal consistency (some combinations are incompatible). 5) Filter by credibility. 6) Cluster surviving configurations into archetypes. 7) Evaluate archetypes.
