# Theory of Constraints Thinking Processes

**One-line summary:** Five-tool diagnostic suite for systemic constraint analysis: Current Reality Tree, Evaporating Cloud (conflict resolution), Future Reality Tree, Prerequisite Tree, Transition Tree. Frames every problem as an undesirable effect rooted in a single binding constraint.

**When to reach for it:** When facing a complex organizational problem with many symptoms ("undesirable effects") and unclear root cause — and when the analyst suspects the symptoms share a single underlying constraint. Distinct from Theory of Constraints' general systems frame (which is about operational throughput); the Thinking Processes are the diagnostic toolkit.

## Purpose

Most organizational problems present as a thicket of symptoms. The TP suite progressively reduces the thicket: CRT finds the core conflict (one constraint generates many UDEs); EC reframes the conflict so both sides' valid concerns can be met; FRT projects the future after the constraint is broken; PRT identifies obstacles to that future; TT sequences the implementation steps.

## How To Use

1. **Current Reality Tree** — List undesirable effects (UDEs); chain backward via cause-effect to find the core problem.
2. **Evaporating Cloud** — Diagram the conflict between two seemingly necessary actions; surface the assumptions that make them seem incompatible; invalidate one assumption to dissolve the conflict.
3. **Future Reality Tree** — Project the chain of consequences from breaking the constraint.
4. **Prerequisite Tree** — Identify obstacles to implementation; for each, identify the intermediate objective that would clear it.
5. **Transition Tree** — Sequence concrete actions.
