# Gadamer Fusion of Horizons

**One-line summary:** Understanding happens when the interpreter's horizon (their formed perspective) meets the text/other's horizon — neither dominates, both shift, a fused horizon emerges.

**When to reach for it:** Reading texts from distant times or cultures; dialogue across deep disagreement; coaching across worldview differences; any context where understanding requires genuine encounter rather than information transfer.

## Purpose

Gadamer rejected the objectivist hermeneutic that aimed to recover the author's original intention as if the interpreter's perspective were a contamination. He argued understanding always involves the interpreter's tradition (their 'horizon') meeting the text's horizon; the encounter changes both. The Fusion is not synthesis or compromise but a genuine new horizon. The discipline is willingness to be changed by the encounter, not just to extract information from it.

## How To Use

1) Acknowledge your own horizon — the prejudices, formations, traditions you bring. 2) Engage the text or other seriously, allowing it to interrogate you. 3) Hold both horizons in awareness without forcing convergence. 4) Notice when the encounter shifts your horizon. 5) Let the new horizon be different from either starting point.
