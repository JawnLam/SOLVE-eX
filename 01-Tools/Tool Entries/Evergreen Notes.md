# Evergreen Notes (Matuschak)

**One-line summary:** A digital-Zettel discipline — notes should be atomic, concept-oriented, densely linked, contextless (work outside their source) — refined over time through re-encounter.

**When to reach for it:** Building a personal knowledge base intended for long-term use; writers who reuse ideas across many works; researchers building cumulative understanding.

## Purpose

Matuschak adapts and extends the Zettelkasten tradition for digital tools. Evergreen Notes have specific principles: atomic (one concept per note), concept-oriented (titled by what the concept is, not what's in source), densely linked (every note connects to many others), contextless (readable independently of source). The discipline matters more than the principles individually — over time, the network develops emergent structure that supports composition and surfacing of latent connections. The principles are well-suited to digital tools (Roam, Obsidian, Logseq).

## How To Use

1) Write notes atomic — one concept per note. 2) Title by what the concept is, not by source or topic. 3) Link extensively to other notes. 4) Make notes contextless — they should be useful without their source. 5) Refine over time — re-encounter triggers updates that improve the note.
