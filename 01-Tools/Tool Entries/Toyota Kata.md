# Toyota Kata (Coaching Kata)

**One-line summary:** A two-part practice — Improvement Kata (a 4-step scientific-method workflow toward a target condition) plus Coaching Kata (a 5-question coaching pattern that develops the practitioner's scientific-thinking habit).

**When to reach for it:** Building improvement capability in organizations; teaching scientific thinking to frontline workers and managers; institutionalizing kaizen as habit rather than event.

## Purpose

Rother studied Toyota and concluded that the company's competitive advantage was less the techniques (kanban, etc.) than the routines of thought their managers practiced. The Improvement Kata is the scientific-method routine: understand the direction, grasp the current condition, establish the next target condition, experiment to close the gap. The Coaching Kata is the manager-practitioner dialogue that develops the routine in the learner. Together they build organizational habit of disciplined experimentation. The method is currently spreading in healthcare, education, and Lean-adjacent contexts.

## How To Use

1) Improvement Kata: (a) understand the direction or challenge, (b) grasp the current condition, (c) establish the next target condition, (d) PDCA toward the target. 2) Coaching Kata: 5 questions asked daily — What's the target? What's the actual condition now? What obstacles? What's your next step? When can we see what we learned?
