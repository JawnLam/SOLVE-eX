# 40 Inventive Principles (Altshuller)

**One-line summary:** Altshuller's 40 patterns of inventive solution — segmentation, asymmetry, prior counteraction, etc. — derived from analysis of millions of patents.

**When to reach for it:** Engineering design contradictions; product redesign; non-engineering domains adapting the principles (TRIZ-for-management, TRIZ-for-service-design).

## Purpose

From patent analysis, Altshuller identified 40 recurring patterns of inventive solution. They are pattern-level prompts: 'segmentation' (divide the system into independent parts), 'taking out' (separate the harmful part), 'preliminary action' (do the action before it's needed), etc. The 40 are paired with the Contradiction Matrix as the routing structure: identify the contradiction parameters, get recommended principles, apply each, choose the best.

## How To Use

1) State the engineering contradiction. 2) Use the Contradiction Matrix or just scan the 40. 3) For each candidate principle, apply it to the specific situation. 4) Generate concrete solution sketches. 5) Evaluate; select the most promising.
