# World Café

**One-line summary:** A large-group dialogue method using small-table rotations on a sequence of questions, with traveling table-hosts cross-pollinating insights.

**When to reach for it:** Strategic conversations with 20-100+ participants; community engagement; multi-stakeholder visioning; cross-functional alignment workshops.

## Purpose

Plenary discussion in large groups produces low participation depth. World Café decomposes the room into rotating small tables, each focused on a specific question, with a host who stays and welcomes successive groups. The method achieves both intimacy (small-table depth) and synthesis (cross-pollination via rotation). Outputs are typically clustered themes from the collective conversation.

## How To Use

1) Choose 3-4 progressive questions. 2) Set up tables of 4-5 with butcher paper. 3) Round 1: discuss question 1 for 20 minutes; one person agrees to host the next round. 4) Rotate guests; host welcomes new guests with prior table's synthesis. 5) Repeat for each question. 6) Plenary harvest the themes.
