# Ho'oponopono

**One-line summary:** Traditional Hawaiian family-based protocol for reconciliation and restoration of relational harmony.

**When to reach for it:** A family or close-knit group has accumulated unresolved hurts, grievances, or tangled obligations that block forward movement and require structured collective repair.

## Purpose

Ho'oponopono ('to set right, to make right') addresses the Hawaiian view that illness, conflict, and stuck circumstances arise from disordered relationships — within self, family, ancestors, and the divine. The protocol surfaces hidden grievances, attributes them without scapegoating, and restores aloha through explicit forgiveness work. It treats relational repair as a cognitive-spiritual technology, not therapy in the Western sense.

## How To Use

A respected facilitator (haku) convenes the family. Steps: (1) Pule — opening prayer setting reverent space. (2) Kūkulu kumuhana — identifying the problem to be addressed. (3) Mahiki — peeling back layers; each participant shares their experience of the trouble without interruption, surfacing entanglements (hihia) and grievances (hala). (4) Mihi — sincere confession and request for forgiveness from each party in turn. (5) Kala — release/cutting of the entanglement by all parties. (6) Pani — closing ritual, often shared meal. Modern variants (Morrnah Simeona) condense to a four-phrase mantra: 'I'm sorry. Please forgive me. Thank you. I love you.' Used as self-administered cognitive practice.
