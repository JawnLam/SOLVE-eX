# Montessori Method

**One-line summary:** A child-led pedagogical method using a structured environment of self-correcting materials, mixed-age classrooms, and sensitive-period developmental sequencing.

**When to reach for it:** Pedagogical design for early childhood and elementary education; alternative-education contexts; principled critique of standard age-graded teacher-led models.

## Purpose

Montessori observed that children at certain developmental periods are intrinsically motivated to learn specific skills. The method designs the environment to provide self-correcting materials at the right developmental moment, allowing children to learn independently through engagement with materials. Teachers are observers and guides rather than instructors. The mixed-age classroom enables peer learning. The method has empirical evidence for cognitive and social-emotional benefits and remains influential in alternative-education traditions.

## How To Use

1) Prepare the environment with developmentally-appropriate self-correcting materials. 2) Group children in mixed-age classrooms. 3) Allow children to choose their own work. 4) Observe rather than instruct. 5) Introduce new materials at moments of developmental readiness. 6) Build the habit of focused independent work.
