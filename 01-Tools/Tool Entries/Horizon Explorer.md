# Horizon Explorer

**One-line summary:** Three-lens futures thinking — possible, probable, preferred — applied across 20+ strategic questions.

**When to reach for it:** Strategic planning that needs to consider multiple future scenarios, not just predictions.

---

## Purpose Of This Thinking Tool

Futures Thinking is a proactive way to envision various scenarios for the future, allowing for strategic planning and action today. It's not about predicting what will happen, but about considering different possibilities and deciding on the best course of action to shape a desired outcome.

Three Perspectives on the Future:
- **Possible Futures:** Envision all potential outcomes, from the ordinary to the extraordinary, that could feasibly happen.
- **Probable Future:** Focus on the most likely future based on current trends and the assumption that not much will change.
- **Preferred Future:** Imagine the ideal future, the one you would want to strive for. This optimistic outlook can inspire innovative ideas and major advances.

## Why Use This Thinking Tool

By regularly contemplating these questions, you or your organization can better prepare for the future, making informed decisions that steer towards success.

## How To Use This Thinking Tool

The concept of "Futures Thinking" is a strategic approach to prepare for the uncertainties of what's ahead. Answer these questions and explore a wide range of future possibilities without limiting your imagination.
