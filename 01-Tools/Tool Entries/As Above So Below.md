# Hermetic As-Above-So-Below Method

**One-line summary:** Hermetic cognitive frame from the Emerald Tablet: when investigating a phenomenon at one scale, search for its analog at another scale — macro-micro correspondences as testable hypotheses.

**When to reach for it:** Investigating a phenomenon at one scale where conventional analytic methods feel exhausted, and where the hypothesis that similar structures operate at different scales might yield insight if applied as a testable heuristic rather than a mystical assertion.

## Purpose

The principle 'that which is above is like to that which is below' comes from the *Tabula Smaragdina* (Emerald Tablet), a foundational text of the Hermetic tradition (~8th c. Arabic origin, later Latin translations). The cognitive method: when investigating a phenomenon at one scale, search for its analog at another scale — the social structure of an organism's cells, the planetary motion of an atom's electrons, the macro-economic patterns of micro-economic decisions. Macrocosm-microcosm correspondences are not assumed but treated as testable hypotheses. Influenced Renaissance natural philosophy (Bruno, Paracelsus, Newton's alchemical work), and indirectly modern systems-thinking and fractal-geometry intuitions.

## How To Use

Identify the phenomenon under investigation at its current scale. Ask: what structure does this exhibit? What relations among parts? What dynamics? Now ask: at what other scale might this structure also appear? Smaller (cellular, atomic, micro-economic) or larger (planetary, civilizational, ecological)? Search for the proposed analog. Test the correspondence: does it actually hold? Does the smaller-scale or larger-scale system exhibit homologous structure? If yes, use the analogy as a source of testable hypotheses about features of the original phenomenon that have not yet been investigated. If no, the correspondence was misguided. The discipline distinguishes Hermetic-as-rigorous from Hermetic-as-mystical assertion.
