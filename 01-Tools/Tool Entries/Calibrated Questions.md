# Calibrated Questions (Voss)

**One-line summary:** Open-ended questions starting with 'how' or 'what' (never 'why') that put the counterparty in problem-solving mode without triggering defensiveness.

**When to reach for it:** Negotiation; difficult requests where 'just ask' would trigger reactance; eliciting cooperation in scenarios where you have less leverage than the counterparty.

## Purpose

Yes/no and 'why' questions trigger defensiveness or rationalization. Calibrated 'how' and 'what' questions ask the counterparty to think with you. 'How can I do that?' shifts the problem to them without confrontation. 'What about this is most important to you?' surfaces priorities without imposing yours. 'How am I supposed to do that?' (a Voss favorite) puts an unreasonable demand back onto the counterparty for them to reconsider. The method is teachable and gives small-leverage parties significant capacity to redirect conversations.

## How To Use

1) Avoid 'why' (triggers defense), 'do you' / 'will you' (yes/no closing). 2) Use 'how' and 'what' starts. 3) Frame questions so the counterparty must think to answer. 4) Specific Voss go-tos: 'How can I do that?', 'What about this is most important to you?', 'How am I supposed to...?', 'How can we solve this problem?'
