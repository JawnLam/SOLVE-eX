# Growth and Underinvestment (Senge Archetype #8)

**One-line summary:** Growth strains capacity; capacity expansion lags; service quality degrades; demand softens; the firm under-invests because growth seemed self-limiting.

**When to reach for it:** Service-business scaling failures; infrastructure-investment under-decisions; education-quality-during-rapid-enrollment-growth patterns; SaaS-product scaling that misses customer-satisfaction degradation.

## Purpose

A subtle variant of Limits to Growth: growth strains capacity (Limits-to-Growth structure), but the symptom of strained capacity (degraded service) reduces demand. The firm reads the demand softening as 'we've hit our market ceiling' and under-invests in capacity, locking in the limit. The fundamental fix (invest ahead of capacity) feels imprudent because the demand signal suggests growth has stopped. The diagnostic is asking whether quality degradation, not market exhaustion, is causing the demand softening.

## How To Use

1) Map growth, capacity strain, quality, demand. 2) Confirm capacity is the binding constraint. 3) Check: is demand softening because of true market exhaustion or capacity-induced quality drop? 4) If quality-induced, invest in capacity even though demand looks soft. 5) Verify that the demand recovers with restored quality.
