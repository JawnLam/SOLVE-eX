# Circle of Competence

**One-line summary:** Know the boundary of what you know — stay inside it for high-stakes decisions; reject opportunities outside it unless their consequences are bounded.

**When to reach for it:** High-stakes investment, hiring, or strategic decisions in unfamiliar domains; resisting FOMO into domains where you can't accurately price risk.

## Purpose

The stance separates 'things I understand well enough to make consequential decisions' from 'things that look exciting but exceed my competence.' Buffett: the size of the circle matters less than its accurate definition. The damage from operating outside the circle is most severe when you don't know you're outside it. The discipline is recognizing the boundary, especially under social/economic pressure to act.

## How To Use

1) For each consequential decision domain, ask: do I know what I don't know? 2) Inventory the specific knowledge that grounds your confidence. 3) Identify the boundary signals (questions you can't answer; analogies that break down). 4) Inside the circle, act with conviction. 5) Outside, either decline or shrink position size dramatically until you're back inside it.
