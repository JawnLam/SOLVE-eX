# MI OARS

**One-line summary:** The four core MI conversation skills — Open questions, Affirmations, Reflective listening, Summarizing.

**When to reach for it:** Motivational Interviewing practice; coaching for behavior-change support; clinical communication training; any conversation where eliciting the other's own motivation matters.

## Purpose

OARS specifies the conversational micro-skills that make MI work. Open questions invite the client to elaborate; Affirmations recognize strengths and effort; Reflective listening checks understanding while deepening exploration; Summaries integrate and refocus. The four skills work together to create a relational atmosphere where the client's own change motivation can emerge. Teaching OARS individually is the standard MI training approach.

## How To Use

1) Open questions: 'What' / 'How' / 'Tell me about...' rather than 'why' or yes/no questions. 2) Affirmations: notice and name client strengths, efforts, intentions. 3) Reflective listening: paraphrase the client's meaning in your own words; offer simple and complex reflections. 4) Summaries: integrate themes from the conversation; orient to next steps.
