# Activation Energy

**One-line summary:** Every transition has an energy threshold — reducing the threshold accelerates desired transitions; raising it suppresses undesired ones.

**When to reach for it:** Designing for habit formation, friction-reduction in workflows, deterrence of unwanted behaviors, change management when desired behaviors lag despite intent.

## Purpose

Chemistry's activation-energy concept transfers to behavior: a desired action that requires significant setup energy will be skipped, even when wanted. Reducing setup friction (pre-staged gym clothes, default-on settings, one-click checkout) raises completion rates more than persuasion does. Conversely, adding friction (cooling-off periods, multi-step confirms) reduces unwanted behaviors more reliably than prohibitions.

## How To Use

1) Identify the desired (or undesired) behavior. 2) Map the friction sequence — what does someone have to do to start? 3) Reduce friction for desired behaviors (defaults, one-tap, pre-staging). 4) Add friction for undesired behaviors (delays, confirmations, gates). 5) Measure conversion at each stage; iterate.
