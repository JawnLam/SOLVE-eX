# Accidental Adversaries (Senge Archetype #9)

**One-line summary:** Two parties with aligned interests end up undermining each other because each takes locally-rational actions that hurt the other's effectiveness.

**When to reach for it:** Partnerships that turn adversarial despite aligned strategic interests; cross-functional team conflicts; vendor-customer relationships that should be cooperative but aren't.

## Purpose

The archetype operates when parties have genuinely aligned interests but each takes actions that locally appear rational while harming the other party's ability to deliver what the first party needs. Each interprets the other's reduced delivery as 'they're not committed' and reduces their own commitment. The relationship deteriorates without any malice. The diagnostic is recognizing that the parties' interests are aligned and the deterioration is structural, not motivational. The intervention requires re-examining each party's locally-rational moves for cross-impact.

## How To Use

1) Verify the parties' interests are genuinely aligned. 2) Map each party's actions and effects on the other. 3) Identify the locally-rational actions that are damaging cross-party effectiveness. 4) Address the structural pattern explicitly; renegotiate based on the shared interest. 5) Build coordination mechanisms that surface cross-impact early.
