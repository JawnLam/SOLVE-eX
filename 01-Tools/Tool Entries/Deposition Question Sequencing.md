# Deposition Question-Sequencing Strategy

**One-line summary:** Strategic sequencing method for deposition questioning — start with foundation/background, progress to context, move to event-specific facts, reserve impeachment for trial.

**When to reach for it:** Conducting a deposition of an adverse or neutral witness where the goals are to lock in admissions, develop evidence for trial, and avoid tipping the witness to impeachment material that should be reserved.

## Purpose

Deposition question-sequencing is the strategic ordering of inquiry to maximize what is locked in and minimize what is tipped. Standard sequence: (1) Foundational/background facts (identity, employment, qualifications) — lock in the witness's narrative arc and self-presentation. (2) Context-of-event (where, when, who was present) — lock in scene-setting. (3) Event-specific facts (what happened, what was said, what was observed) — pin admissions, force commitments to specific accounts. (4) Documentary review (using exhibits) — extract admissions tied to documents. (5) Conclude. Critically: impeachment material (prior inconsistent statements, contradictory documents) is generally NOT brought out in deposition — it is reserved for trial impact. Bringing it out in deposition lets the witness explain or repair, blunting trial use.

## How To Use

Prepare an outline organized by topic, not by document — topical organization controls flow. Within each topic, sequence general-to-specific. Begin with foundational facts; you want the witness comfortable and committed to their self-presentation before you reach contested questions. Move to context, then event-specifics. Use exhibits sparingly — each exhibit shown is an exhibit the witness can prepare for at trial. For each event-specific area, ask the question, lock in the answer with follow-ups, then move on. Resist the temptation to argue with the witness or display contrary evidence. The deposition's strategic purpose is to gather and lock; the trial's purpose is to use the gathered material with impact. Keep impeachment ammunition holstered.
