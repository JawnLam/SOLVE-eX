# Medieval Fourfold Sense (Quadriga)

**One-line summary:** Read scripture across four senses — literal (history), allegorical (Christ-typology), moral (tropological), anagogical (eschatological) — as different valid layers.

**When to reach for it:** Christian scriptural exegesis in traditions that maintain the fourfold reading; comparative reading across hermeneutic traditions; teaching contemplative engagement with sacred text.

## Purpose

The Quadriga is the Christian sister tradition to Jewish Pardes — different specific levels, similar structural insight that sacred text supports multiple coexisting readings. The four are not alternatives; they are co-present. Cassian and later medievals organized them: literal grounds historical events; allegorical reads Old Testament events as types of Christ; tropological extracts moral instruction; anagogical points toward the eschatological end. The mnemonic 'Littera gesta docet, quid credas allegoria, moralis quid agas, quo tendas anagogia' summarizes the discipline.

## How To Use

1) Read the literal-historical level: what happened, what does the text say. 2) Read the allegorical level: how does this prefigure Christ or the Church. 3) Read the tropological / moral level: what should the reader do. 4) Read the anagogical level: what does this point to in eschatological time. 5) Hold all four together without forcing reduction.
