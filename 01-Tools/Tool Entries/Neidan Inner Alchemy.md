# Neidan (Inner Alchemy)

**One-line summary:** Daoist esoteric system combining breath, visualization, posture, and symbolic-cosmological framing (Five Phases, Yijing trigrams) to refine essence-qi-spirit (jing-qi-shen) into spiritual immortality. Multi-stage practice spanning years to decades.

**When to reach for it:** Within Daoist contemplative-somatic lineages with qualified teachers. Not a self-administered method — the practice has subtle physiological and psychological dimensions that require guidance. Engaged with respectfully and over long timescales.

## Purpose

Daoist neidan inherits the language of external alchemy (laboratory transmutation of substances) and applies it to the body's subtle physiology. The practitioner refines coarser substances (jing, essence/seminal energy) into subtler ones (qi, vital energy) and finally into shen (spirit). The goal varies by lineage — longevity, immortality, return-to-Dao — but the cognitive payload is access to states and capacities ordinary in waking life are not.

## How To Use

Neidan practice is lineage-specific and requires guidance. General structural pattern:
1. **Foundational practices** — quiet sitting (jing zuo), breath regulation, basic qigong.
2. **Foundation building** (zhuji) — accumulate jing and qi through specific practices.
3. **Refining jing to qi** — transmute denser energy to subtler.
4. **Refining qi to shen** — transmute vital energy to spiritual.
5. **Refining shen to emptiness** — return to the source.
6. **Throughout** — work with specific symbolic-cosmological maps (Five Phases, Yijing trigrams, internal viscera correspondences).

Find a qualified teacher; this entry is descriptive, not instructional.
