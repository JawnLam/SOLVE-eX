# IFS 6Fs Unblending Protocol

**One-line summary:** A 6-step protocol for accessing and unblending from an active part — Find, Focus, Flesh out, Feel toward, beFriend, fears.

**When to reach for it:** Therapy or self-work where a strong part is dominating consciousness and the practitioner needs structured access; teaching IFS to clients or coaching trainees.

## Purpose

The 6Fs are the canonical access protocol in Internal Family Systems. When a part is blended (dominant in awareness), Self cannot relate to it from a curious or compassionate stance. The 6Fs sequence creates space: Find the part in body or thought, Focus attention on it, Flesh out its details, Feel toward it (notice Self's response), beFriend it (build relationship), and ask its fears (the function it serves). The protocol is teachable and reproducible.

## How To Use

1) Find — notice where in the body or mind the part shows up. 2) Focus — direct attention there. 3) Flesh out — describe what you see/feel about it. 4) Feel toward — check how you (Self) feel toward it. 5) beFriend — communicate care for the part. 6) Ask its fears — what is it protecting against?
