# Trends of Engineering Evolution (TRIZ)

**One-line summary:** A catalog of named patterns engineered systems follow as they evolve — increasing ideality, mono-bi-poly transition, dynamization, transition to micro-scale, etc.

**When to reach for it:** Roadmap planning; identifying which next-step transitions are likely in a technology family; competitive analysis of where rivals are likely to push.

## Purpose

Altshuller observed that engineered systems evolve along a small number of recurring trajectories. The catalog (originally 8, now 15-20 in modern TRIZ practice) names each: 'increasing ideality' (more function, less mass/cost/energy); 'mono-bi-poly' (single -> dual -> multi-element systems); 'dynamization' (rigid -> flexible -> liquid -> field); 'transition to micro-scale'; etc. Knowing the trends provides forecasting power: where is this technology likely to go next?

## How To Use

1) Identify the current system on the relevant trends. 2) For each trend, ask: where is the system on this trend, and what's the next step? 3) Identify viable next-step variants. 4) Prioritize by feasibility and strategic value. 5) Roadmap.
