# Lōkahi Triangle

**One-line summary:** Hawaiian three-force harmony frame (God/Akua – Nature/'Āina – Humans/Kanaka) used as a diagnostic for imbalance.

**When to reach for it:** Diagnosing personal, communal, or ecological dis-ease and locating which relational corner has fallen out of harmony.

## Purpose

The Lōkahi Triangle treats wholeness as the equal tensioning of three relational fields — vertical (with Akua/the sacred), lateral-ecological (with 'Āina/place), and horizontal-social (with Kanaka/people). Illness, conflict, or stuck circumstances are read as one corner pulling against the others. Restoring lōkahi requires not addressing the symptom but re-aligning the imbalanced corner with the other two.

## How To Use

Sketch the triangle with each corner labeled. For the current trouble, ask: which corner is over-emphasized? Under-attended? In conflict with another? Trace recent decisions, attentions, and time-allocations against each corner. Identify the corner most starved of attention. Choose a small, concrete practice that restores that corner (a walk on the land, a prayer, a relational repair). Re-assess after a week. The diagnostic is restorative, not analytic — it asks what re-balancing the situation needs, not what's wrong with it.
