# Behavior Change Wheel (Michie)

**One-line summary:** A comprehensive framework wrapping COM-B with 9 intervention functions and 7 policy categories — the most complete behavior-change-intervention design tool.

**When to reach for it:** Public-health or organizational behavior-change program design; policy intervention selection; ensuring that intervention choice systematically considers all options.

## Purpose

The Behaviour Change Wheel layers three concentric rings: COM-B at the center (diagnose deficit), intervention functions (educate / persuade / incentivize / coerce / train / restrict / environmental restructure / model / enable) in the middle, and policy categories (communication / guidelines / fiscal / regulation / legislation / environmental planning / service provision) outermost. The diagnostic flows: identify COM-B deficit, choose intervention function that addresses it, choose policy category that delivers the function. The framework is now standard in UK public-health intervention design.

## How To Use

1) Identify the target behavior. 2) Use COM-B to identify deficits. 3) Map deficits to intervention functions (e.g., capability deficits suggest education or training). 4) Map functions to policy categories. 5) Design specific interventions within the chosen function and category. 6) Evaluate using the same framework.
