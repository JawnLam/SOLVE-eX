# Tactical Empathy (Voss)

**One-line summary:** Demonstrably accurate empathy under adversarial conditions — labeling emotions, mirroring, and asking calibrated questions to lower defenses and surface counterparty interests.

**When to reach for it:** High-stakes negotiation; adversarial conversations where rapport must be built without conceding position; hostage-negotiation-grade communication training.

## Purpose

Voss developed tactical empathy in FBI hostage negotiation. Standard negotiation training emphasizes interests and BATNA; Voss adds psychological depth: counterparties under stress are not in optimization mode but in emotional defense, and surfacing/labeling their emotional state is the precondition for any rational engagement. Tactical empathy operationalizes this: demonstrate accurate understanding of the counterparty's emotional reality, lower their defenses, and only then probe interests. The method scales to business negotiation and difficult conversations.

## How To Use

1) Listen for the counterparty's emotional state. 2) Label it explicitly ('It seems like you're feeling X because Y'). 3) Mirror — repeat back their last 1-3 words. 4) Use calibrated 'how' and 'what' questions to invite elaboration. 5) Build a 'no' early to reduce reactance. 6) Only after rapport is established, probe interests and propose options.
