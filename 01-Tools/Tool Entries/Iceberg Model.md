# Iceberg Model

**One-line summary:** Read a system at four nested levels — events / patterns / structures / mental models — with the deeper levels having more leverage.

**When to reach for it:** Diagnosis of recurring organizational problems; sense-making workshops; teaching systems thinking; moving beyond event-reactive management.

## Purpose

Iceberg pedagogy resists event-level intervention by making the deeper layers visible. Events (what happened) are the tip; below are patterns over time (what keeps happening), then structures (what generates the patterns), then mental models (what makes the structures look reasonable). High-leverage intervention happens at the lower levels, but most attention defaults to the tip. The tool is the recurring drill from event to mental model.

## How To Use

1) For a recurring problem, name a specific event. 2) Ask: is this a pattern? List similar past events. 3) Ask: what structures (incentives, rules, information flows) generate the pattern? 4) Ask: what mental models make those structures feel normal? 5) Intervene at the deepest accessible level.
