# Bhuta Shuddhi (Elemental Purification)

**One-line summary:** Tantric pre-practice preparation — systematic cleansing of the five elements in the body through pranayama, mantra, and visualization.

**When to reach for it:** Preparing the body-mind for extended contemplative or ritual practice and seeking a structured method for somatic-energetic settling rather than ad-hoc relaxation.

## Purpose

Bhuta Shuddhi ('element purification') is a tantric preparation in which the practitioner systematically dissolves each of the five elements (earth, water, fire, air, ether) in the body into the next-subtler one, then back into pure awareness, before manifesting them again in purified form. The cognitive-energetic payload: each element corresponds to body regions, sensations, and emotional textures; the dissolution-and-reconstitution clears habitual patterns and prepares the practitioner for subsequent practice. Used before yantra worship, deity invocation, or extended meditation.

## How To Use

Sit comfortably with attention to breath. Begin with awareness of the earth element (solid-feeling body parts, especially below the navel). With breath and visualization, dissolve earth into water (sensation of moisture, fluidity, sweat). Dissolve water into fire (warmth, vitality, digestion). Dissolve fire into air (breath, mobility, lightness). Dissolve air into ether (space, openness, sound). Dissolve ether into pure awareness. Rest. Then re-manifest each element in reverse order, this time as luminous and purified. Use accompanying mantras and bija-syllables from the tradition. Takes 20-45 minutes; deepens with practice.
