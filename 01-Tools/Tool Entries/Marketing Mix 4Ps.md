# Marketing Mix 4Ps

**One-line summary:** Plan a marketing approach across four levers — Product, Price, Place, Promotion — coordinating decisions across them rather than in isolation.

**When to reach for it:** Product-launch planning; campaign design; aligning marketing decisions across functions; teaching the basic structure of marketing strategy.

## Purpose

The 4Ps decompose the marketing decision into orthogonal axes so each can be specified explicitly and coordinated with the others. The framework prevents siloed decisions (great product, wrong price; right channel, wrong promotion). Extensions (7Ps for services, etc.) exist but the canonical 4 remain pedagogically dominant.

## How To Use

1) Product: what is being offered, with what features and quality? 2) Price: at what price point, with what discounts, payment terms? 3) Place: through what channels does it reach the customer? 4) Promotion: how is awareness and intent built? 5) Check coordination — does the price match the positioning? does the channel match the segment?
