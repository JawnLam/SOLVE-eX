# Weekly Review (GTD)

**One-line summary:** A weekly recurring review — clear the inbox, process to zero, review next-actions lists, look ahead to upcoming commitments, surface things that fell through.

**When to reach for it:** Sustaining trust in any personal productivity system over time; preventing system decay; re-orienting after a busy or chaotic week.

## Purpose

Personal productivity systems decay without maintenance. Allen's Weekly Review is the keystone discipline that keeps the system trustworthy: catch up on capture, process all open items, review all lists, look ahead at calendar, surface anything missed. Without the Review, the system becomes unreliable, the practitioner loses trust in it, and capture decays. Practitioners report that maintaining the Weekly Review is the single highest-impact GTD habit.

## How To Use

1) Schedule a recurring 1-2 hour block each week. 2) Get to inbox zero. 3) Review project list — is each project actionable? 4) Review next-action lists — anything to add, complete, or remove? 5) Review calendar for past week (anything dropped?) and upcoming weeks. 6) Surface any commitments not yet captured. 7) End with the system trusted and current.
