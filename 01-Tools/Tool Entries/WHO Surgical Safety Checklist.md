# WHO Surgical Safety Checklist

**One-line summary:** WHO-developed 19-item checklist with three critical pauses — Sign-In before anesthesia, Time-Out before incision, Sign-Out before leaving OR — shown to reduce surgical complications and mortality globally.

**When to reach for it:** Performing surgical procedures of any complexity where the well-documented failure modes (wrong-site surgery, antibiotic omission, retained instruments, mis-identification) can be prevented by structured pauses.

## Purpose

The WHO Surgical Safety Checklist (2008) is a 19-item checklist developed to standardize critical communication and verification across surgical teams worldwide. Three critical pauses: Sign-In (before anesthesia induction) verifies patient identity, consent, allergies, anticipated airway risk, blood loss risk; Time-Out (immediately before skin incision) requires team introductions, antibiotic timing confirmation, anticipated critical events from surgeon/anesthesia/nursing; Sign-Out (before patient leaves OR) verifies instrument counts, specimen labeling, equipment problems, key concerns for recovery. The Haynes et al. (2009) NEJM study showed reduced surgical mortality and complications across eight hospitals globally.

## How To Use

The checklist is implemented as physical or digital document; a designated checklist coordinator (often the circulating nurse) leads the pauses. Sign-In: before anesthesia, the coordinator asks the team to confirm patient identity, surgical site marked, anesthesia safety check, pulse oximeter, allergies, difficult airway risk, blood loss risk. Time-Out: immediately before skin incision, all team members pause. Team members introduce themselves and their roles. Surgeon confirms patient, procedure, site. Anesthesia confirms safety. Nurse confirms instrument count and sterility. Antibiotic timing verified. Anticipated critical events discussed. Sign-Out: before patient leaves OR, instrument counts verified, specimens labeled, equipment problems noted, key concerns for recovery communicated.
