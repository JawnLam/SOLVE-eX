# Welcoming Prayer (Mary Mrozowski)

**One-line summary:** Active companion to Centering Prayer developed by Mary Mrozowski (1990s, Contemplative Outreach). When emotionally-charged sensation arises during daily life, three steps: Focus and sink into it, Welcome it, Let go.

**When to reach for it:** Throughout daily life — at the moment an emotionally-charged reaction arises (anger, fear, shame, grasping). The practice integrates contemplative consent into the texture of ordinary activity, not just sitting practice.

## Purpose

Centering Prayer trains consent to presence in silent sitting. The Welcoming Prayer extends that consent into reactive moments. By bypassing the impulse to suppress or analyze, the practice metabolizes the reaction at its somatic root. "Welcome" doesn't mean agreement with the emotion — it means agreement with reality as it is, including the reaction.

## How To Use

Three steps (originally Mary Mrozowski's):
1. **Focus and sink into the feeling.** Bring full attention to the physical sensation in the body. Don't analyze; just feel.
2. **Welcome the feeling.** Silently say "welcome" — to fear, anger, shame, grasping. Or "yes" or "I consent to the presence of God in this feeling." This is consent to reality, not endorsement of the emotion.
3. **Let go**: Internal phrase such as "I let go of my desire to control this. I let go of my desire to be approved of. I let go of my desire to be safe." These are the three "affective programs for happiness" Keating identified as the unconscious drivers of reactive emotion.
