# Mirroring + Labeling (Voss negotiation micro-skills)

**One-line summary:** Two foundational Voss techniques — mirror the last 1-3 words of what they said; label their emotions explicitly ('It seems like you're feeling X').

**When to reach for it:** Any negotiation or difficult conversation where the counterparty's perspective must be surfaced; coaching for active-listening practice.

## Purpose

Mirroring (repeating the last few words as a question) invites elaboration — the counterparty fills the silence with more information. Labeling (naming the apparent emotion) reduces the emotion's intensity (the affect-labeling effect documented in neuroscience). Together they are the workhorse micro-skills of tactical empathy: in any conversation where you want more information and less defensiveness, mirror and label. They are also low-risk: a mislabeled emotion gets corrected, providing information; a successful label builds rapport.

## How To Use

1) Mirror: when the counterparty stops talking, repeat their last 1-3 words with rising intonation. 2) Wait — let them elaborate. 3) Label: notice an apparent emotional state. 4) Say 'It seems like you're feeling X' or 'It sounds like X is important to you.' 5) Wait for confirmation or correction. 6) Use the new information.
