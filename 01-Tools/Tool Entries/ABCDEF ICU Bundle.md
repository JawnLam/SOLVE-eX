# ABCDEF Bundle (ICU Liberation)

**One-line summary:** Daily ICU multi-component practice bundle — Assess pain, Both spontaneous awakening/breathing trials, Choice of sedation, Delirium assessment, Early mobility, Family engagement.

**When to reach for it:** Managing patients in the intensive care unit where the goal is to reduce delirium, ICU-acquired weakness, and post-ICU syndrome through a coordinated daily multi-disciplinary practice.

## Purpose

The ABCDEF Bundle is the central practice of ICU Liberation, developed by E. Wesley Ely and colleagues through the Society of Critical Care Medicine. Each letter is a daily care element: (A)ssess, prevent, and manage pain; (B)oth spontaneous awakening trials (off sedation) and spontaneous breathing trials (off mechanical ventilator); (C)hoice of analgesia and sedation; (D)elirium assessment, prevention, and management; (E)arly mobility and exercise; (F)amily engagement and empowerment. The bundle is not optional individual elements — research shows the effect comes from doing all elements daily, every day, as multidisciplinary practice. Reduces delirium, ICU mortality, and post-ICU syndrome.

## How To Use

As multidisciplinary daily ICU round practice: for each patient, the team walks through each letter. Pain (A): is pain assessed with validated tool (BPS, CPOT)? Is current analgesia adequate? Both trials (B): can the patient have a spontaneous awakening trial today? A spontaneous breathing trial? Choice of sedation (C): is sedation goal-directed and at lightest level that maintains comfort? Delirium (D): is CAM-ICU or ICDSC performed? Are non-pharmacological prevention measures in place? Early mobility (E): can the patient mobilize today, even ventilated? Family (F): are family engaged in care planning and decision-making? Document each element; address barriers immediately. The discipline is the daily-every-element practice, not the individual letters.
