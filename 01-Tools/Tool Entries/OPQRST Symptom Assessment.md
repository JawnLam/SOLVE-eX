# OPQRST Symptom Assessment

**One-line summary:** OPQRST — Onset, Provocation/Palliation, Quality, Region/Radiation, Severity, Time — is a six-prompt mnemonic for eliciting a complete symptom history under time pressure.

**When to reach for it:** When a patient presents with a symptom (pain, dyspnea, dizziness, palpitations) and the clinician needs a fast, complete, comparable history without missing pivotal qualifiers.

## Purpose

Generates the "semantic qualifiers" — sudden vs gradual, sharp vs dull, radiating vs localized — that organize a differential diagnosis. Without these qualifiers, even an exhaustive question list fails to produce a discriminating clinical picture. OPQRST's discipline is that it forces the *quality* of the symptom into the history, not just its presence.

## How To Use

1. **Onset** — When did it start? What were you doing?
2. **Provocation/Palliation** — What makes it worse? What makes it better?
3. **Quality** — How would you describe it? (sharp, dull, burning, crushing…)
4. **Region/Radiation** — Where is it? Does it move anywhere?
5. **Severity** — On a 0-10 scale, how bad is it now? At its worst?
6. **Time** — Constant or intermittent? How long does it last?
