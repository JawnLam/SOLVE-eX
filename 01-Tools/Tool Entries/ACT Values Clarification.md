# ACT Values Clarification

**One-line summary:** Structured exercises (e.g., Bull's Eye, valued domains card sort, eulogy reflection) to identify what the client wants their life to stand for.

**When to reach for it:** ACT therapy values phase; coaching contexts where clarity about meaningful direction is the bottleneck; transition periods (career, relationship, life-stage).

## Purpose

ACT distinguishes values (chosen life directions) from goals (specific achievable endpoints). Values are not feelings, not goals, not rules — they are stated directions of action over time. Clarification helps clients identify their values in specific life domains (family, work, learning, health, leisure). The clarity then anchors committed action: behavior aligns with values even when motivation, feelings, or circumstances are unfavorable. Multiple specific exercises exist within the broader practice.

## How To Use

1) Identify life domains relevant to the client (typically 8-12). 2) Use specific exercises: Bull's Eye (rate current alignment), Card Sort (prioritize values), Funeral / Eulogy exercise (imagine future legacy). 3) Articulate values as ongoing directions, not achievement endpoints. 4) Translate to committed-action steps in each domain.
