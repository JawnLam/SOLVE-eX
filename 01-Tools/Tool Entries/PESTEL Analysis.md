# STEEP / PESTEL Analysis

**One-line summary:** A scan of the macro-environment across six (or more) dimensions — Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Environmental, Legal — to identify external forces.

**When to reach for it:** Strategic-planning kickoff; market-entry analysis; periodic environmental scanning; preparing input for scenario construction.

## Purpose

The PESTEL framework (and variants STEEP, STEEPLE, PEST) provides a structured checklist for environmental scanning. Without a framework, scanning is unsystematic and tends to miss dimensions. PESTEL ensures each macro-dimension is considered: Political (regulation, stability), Economic (growth, interest rates), Social (demographics, attitudes), Technological (innovation), Environmental (sustainability, climate), Legal (compliance). The output feeds strategic planning, scenario construction, and risk identification.

## How To Use

1) For each PESTEL dimension, brainstorm relevant factors affecting the focal question. 2) Rank factors by impact and likelihood. 3) Identify high-impact factors as strategic drivers. 4) Pass to scenario construction or risk analysis. 5) Re-scan periodically as the environment shifts.
