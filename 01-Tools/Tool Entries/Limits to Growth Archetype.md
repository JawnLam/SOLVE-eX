# Limits to Growth (Senge Archetype #1)

**One-line summary:** A reinforcing growth loop bumps into a balancing limit loop; growth slows or reverses as the limit is approached.

**When to reach for it:** Growth strategies that plateau unexpectedly; ecological carrying-capacity analysis; team-scaling dynamics; market saturation; any system where unconstrained growth eventually hits a constraint.

## Purpose

The first and most pervasive of Senge's archetypes: most reinforcing growth processes operate in the presence of a balancing process (resource depletion, market saturation, organizational capacity). The growth dynamic looks linear in the early phase, masking the eventual constraint. Strategically: growth-only thinking neglects the limit, so the system surprises when growth slows. The diagnostic is identifying the limit early; the intervention is operating on the limiting process, not pushing harder on the growth process.

## How To Use

1) Identify the reinforcing growth loop and the balancing limit loop. 2) Estimate when growth will hit the limit. 3) Choose: (a) work on the limit (e.g., add capacity), or (b) cap growth at a sustainable rate. 4) Avoid the futile attempt to push harder on growth — that accelerates hitting the limit. 5) Re-check periodically as the system evolves.
