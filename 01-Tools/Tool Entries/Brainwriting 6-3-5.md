# Brainwriting 6-3-5 (Rohrbach)

**One-line summary:** 6 people, 3 ideas, 5 minutes per round, 5 rounds with passing — produces 90 ideas in ~30 minutes via structured parallel writing.

**When to reach for it:** Time-constrained group ideation; ideation cultures where verbal brainstorming over-rewards loud voices; need for high-volume idea output.

## Purpose

Rohrbach's structured variant of slip-writing: each of 6 participants writes 3 ideas on a sheet, then passes the sheet to the next person, who builds on the prior ideas or adds new ones. Five rounds produce 90 ideas (6 x 3 x 5) systematically. The 'build on prior' mechanic combines independent ideation with sequential elaboration. The fixed time and count force productivity.

## How To Use

1) Sit 6 people around a table. 2) Each gets a sheet divided into 18 cells (3 cols x 6 rows). 3) Round 1: each writes 3 ideas in row 1 in 5 min. 4) Pass left. 5) Round 2: each reads the prior ideas, writes 3 more (new or building on) in row 2. 6) Continue 5 rounds. 7) Affinity-diagram the 90 ideas.
