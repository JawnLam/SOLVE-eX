# Liquid Democracy (Delegative Voting)

**One-line summary:** Hybrid direct/representative method where voters can vote directly OR delegate their vote to a trusted proxy for specific issues. Proxies can re-delegate, creating chains.

**When to reach for it:** Large-group decision-making at scales where direct voting is infeasible (everyone votes on everything) but representation is too coarse (one rep handles everything). Used in Pirate Parties' governance, DAO experiments, large research collaboratives.

## Purpose

Direct democracy doesn't scale; representative democracy concentrates decisions in a few representatives whose preferences don't match constituents on every issue. Liquid democracy lets a voter delegate per-issue to someone they trust on *that* topic — and reclaim the vote any time. The result is dynamic representation that approximates direct democracy at scale.

## How To Use

1. **Each voter has one vote per issue.**
2. **Default: delegate to a trusted proxy** on a topic (or set of topics).
3. **Proxy can re-delegate** — "transitive delegation" — creating chains.
4. **Any voter can override** at any time, voting directly on a specific issue.
5. **Tally** — direct votes plus delegated chains; proxies hold weight equal to their followers' votes.
6. **Topic-specific delegation** — different proxies for different domains (one for tech, one for ethics, etc.).
