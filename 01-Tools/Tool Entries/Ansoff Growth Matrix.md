# Ansoff Growth Matrix

**One-line summary:** Plot growth options on existing-vs-new product × existing-vs-new market axes: Market Penetration, Market Development, Product Development, Diversification.

**When to reach for it:** Strategic-planning workshops on where to grow; comparing growth options with different risk profiles.

## Purpose

The matrix forces explicit consideration of the four canonical growth vectors and pairs each with its risk profile. Market Penetration (current product, current market) is lowest-risk; Diversification (new product, new market) is highest-risk. The tool prevents the default 'pursue everything' answer and surfaces the risk implications of each chosen vector.

## How To Use

1) Locate the firm at the intersection of current products and current markets. 2) For each growth option under consideration, place it in one of the four quadrants. 3) Discuss risk and capability implications of each. 4) Choose vectors that match the firm's risk tolerance and capability profile. 5) Sequence the chosen vectors to manage cumulative risk.
