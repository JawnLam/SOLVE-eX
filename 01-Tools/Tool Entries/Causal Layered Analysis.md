# Causal Layered Analysis (Inayatullah)

**One-line summary:** Four-layer analytical method for unpacking futures: (1) Litany — official surface, (2) Systemic — underlying causes, (3) Worldview — paradigmatic frame, (4) Myth/Metaphor — deep narrative.

**When to reach for it:** Futures workshops where surface-level trend analysis has reached its limit, and the analyst suspects that the question's depth lies in unexamined cultural narratives or worldview assumptions. Strong for civilizational and culturally-loaded foresight work.

## Purpose

Trend analysis tells you *what* might happen. CLA asks *why this trend appears credible to us*. By descending through four layers — official narrative, systemic mechanism, worldview, foundational myth — the analyst surfaces the cultural assumptions that make a future imaginable or unthinkable. Different layers admit different interventions: changing the litany is easy and shallow; shifting myth is hard and deep.

## How To Use

Work an issue through four layers:
1. **Litany** — The official, surface-level view as it appears in headlines, statistics, public discourse.
2. **Systemic** — The underlying social/economic/political/ecological systems generating the litany.
3. **Worldview** — The paradigm (industrial, ecological, post-secular, transhumanist…) within which the systems are framed.
4. **Myth/Metaphor** — The foundational narrative (progress as conquest, body as machine, time as linear arrow, growth as good).

For strategic intervention: identify which layer is most leverage-rich. Reconstruct alternative futures by varying the deeper layers.
