# Success to the Successful (Senge Archetype #4)

**One-line summary:** Two competitors share a limited resource; whichever pulls ahead early gets disproportionate continued resources, locking in the imbalance.

**When to reach for it:** Allocation dynamics between competing programs, products, or people; tracking decisions in schools; resource-flow patterns in organizations; rich-get-richer dynamics.

## Purpose

Whenever success in early stages produces increased resources, those resources reinforce continued success while the unselected option's deteriorating resources reinforce its decline. The archetype operates on top of any underlying small initial difference, often produced by chance or evaluator bias. Strategically: the resource-allocation rules generate the pattern more than any underlying ability difference does. Intervening means changing the allocation rules to break the runaway, or accepting and protecting the disadvantaged option.

## How To Use

1) Identify the two competing entities and the shared resource. 2) Observe whether allocation responds to performance. 3) If yes, the archetype is operative. 4) Decide: is the runaway dynamic appropriate or counterproductive? 5) If counterproductive, modify allocation rules to dampen runaway. 6) Consider protected allocation for the disadvantaged option.
