# Priority-Based Requirements Matrix

**One-line summary:** 0–3 classification scheme: Not Required / Low Priority / Top Priority / Mandatory.

**When to reach for it:** Many requirements competing for attention; need clarity on what must be in vs. nice-to-have.

---

## Purpose Of This Thinking Tool

The purpose of the "Priority-Based Requirements Matrix" thinking tool is to methodically organize and prioritize the different elements of a project or idea. It's particularly useful in creative and innovative processes where clear documentation and understanding of required outcomes are essential for success. This tool assists in:

1. **Identifying Essential Elements:** Determining which components are absolutely necessary for the project's success.
2. **Prioritizing Components:** Ranking elements based on their importance, utility, and relevance to the core objectives.
3. **Distinguishing Between Wants and Needs:** Differentiating between essential elements and those that are merely desirable.
4. **Eliminating Unnecessary Elements:** Identifying aspects that are not required or could potentially hinder the project.
5. **Facilitating Team Alignment:** Ensuring all team members have a shared understanding of what is crucial, beneficial, or irrelevant to the project.

## Why Use This Thinking Tool

Documenting the expected results or products of a project is a crucial but often neglected step in creative and innovative processes. Without a clear understanding and agreement on what the final requirements are, it's challenging to generate effective ideas or solutions.

## How To Use This Thinking Tool

If the categories provided don't suit your project's needs, feel free to create your own classification system. It can be helpful to prioritize or rank the requirements within each category you define, to give clarity on their importance or order of implementation.

## The Actual Thinking Tool

Classify the elements of your idea or solution based on their importance or necessity.

```
|=============|==============|======================================================================================|
| Requirement | Requirement  |                                                                                      |
|    Level    |     Type     |                                     Description                                      |
|=============|==============|======================================================================================|
|           3 | Mandatory    | These are essential elements that must be included in any proposed idea or solution. |
|           2 | Top Priority | Elements that are highly beneficial and should ideally be included.                  |
|           1 | Low Priority | Elements that are desirable but not essential ("wants" rather than "needs").         |
|           0 | Not Required | Elements that should not be included in the proposed idea or solution.               |
|=============|==============|======================================================================================|

|==============================|=============|
```
