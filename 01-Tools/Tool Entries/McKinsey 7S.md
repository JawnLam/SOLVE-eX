# McKinsey 7S

**One-line summary:** Assess organizational alignment across seven dimensions: Strategy, Structure, Systems, Shared values, Style, Staff, Skills.

**When to reach for it:** Post-merger integration; strategic transformation diagnostics; assessment of whether a planned strategy is operationally supported by the rest of the organization.

## Purpose

Strategic plans often fail because the organization isn't aligned to execute them — structures, systems, and skills lag the strategic intent. McKinsey 7S forces explicit assessment of each dimension and identifies misalignments. The 'soft' Ss (Shared values, Style, Staff, Skills) are typically the lagging axes and are the focus for integration work. The tool is the systematic seven-axis scan.

## How To Use

1) For each S, describe the current state. 2) For each S, describe the state required by the strategy. 3) Identify gaps. 4) Prioritize gap-closing interventions — typically the soft Ss take longest. 5) Re-assess periodically.
