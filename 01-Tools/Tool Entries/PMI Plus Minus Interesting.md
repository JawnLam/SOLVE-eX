# PMI (Plus / Minus / Interesting)

**One-line summary:** Briefly enumerate Pluses, Minuses, and Interestings of an option before judging — counteracts the snap-yes/snap-no reflex.

**When to reach for it:** Quick decisions where the snap reaction is dominating analysis; teaching deliberate thinking to novices; pre-meeting personal preparation on agenda items.

## Purpose

Snap judgments often anchor on one salient feature and miss other relevant considerations. PMI's three-column scan is a deliberate detour: list pluses (whatever the snap valence); list minuses (whatever the snap valence); list interestings (neither plus nor minus, just notable). The Interesting column is the key innovation — it catches considerations that don't fit a binary valence but might matter.

## How To Use

1) State the option. 2) List Pluses for ~2 minutes. 3) List Minuses for ~2 minutes. 4) List Interestings for ~2 minutes. 5) Re-evaluate the snap judgment in light of the three lists. 6) Commit or defer.
