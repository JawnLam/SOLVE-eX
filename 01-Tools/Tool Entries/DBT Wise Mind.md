# DBT Wise Mind

**One-line summary:** The integrative dialectic between emotion-mind and reason-mind — a stance of grounded knowing that incorporates both feeling and analysis.

**When to reach for it:** Decisions under emotional intensity; teaching DBT clients to access integrated decision-making; recurring practice for psychological flexibility.

## Purpose

DBT identifies three states of mind: Emotion Mind (decisions made under emotional dominance), Reasonable Mind (decisions made under cold analysis), and Wise Mind (decisions that integrate both). Wise Mind is not a balance point but a synthesis — it draws on emotional information and rational analysis without being captured by either. The practice is teachable: through breath, body awareness, and explicit checking ('does this feel right AND make sense?'), clients access Wise Mind reliably.

## How To Use

1) Notice which mind state you're in (Emotion / Reasonable / Wise). 2) Breathe and settle. 3) Ask: does this feel right? Does it also make sense? 4) Wait for the integrated knowing. 5) Act from that ground. 6) Practice as a recurring discipline.
