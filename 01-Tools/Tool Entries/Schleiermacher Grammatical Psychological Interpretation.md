# Schleiermacher Grammatical + Psychological Interpretation

**One-line summary:** Interpret a text via two coordinated axes — grammatical (linguistic, contextual, structural) and psychological (empathic reconstruction of the author's mental act).

**When to reach for it:** Close reading of texts where authorial intent matters; biblical exegesis; literary interpretation; legal-document analysis where both syntax and the author's project must be considered.

## Purpose

Schleiermacher developed hermeneutics as a general discipline by identifying its two coordinated axes. Grammatical interpretation works on the text as a piece of language: vocabulary, syntax, genre, historical linguistic context. Psychological interpretation works on the text as a product of an author's mind: the project the author was pursuing, the specific mental act that produced this expression. Neither axis is sufficient alone; both inform each other. The method gives hermeneutics its enduring two-axis structure.

## How To Use

1) Grammatical pass: analyze the text linguistically — vocabulary, syntax, genre conventions, historical language context. 2) Psychological pass: reconstruct the author's project, the mental act producing this text, the author's intellectual context. 3) Cross-check: do the two reconstructions cohere? 4) Iterate as new evidence emerges in either axis.
