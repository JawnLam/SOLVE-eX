# Experimental Innovation Cycle

**One-line summary:** 11-stage experimentation framework treating failure as data — vision → learning agenda → hypothesis → design → run → analyze → integrate → improve.

**When to reach for it:** Need to drive innovation systematically rather than via flashes of insight; willing to fail forward.

---

## Purpose Of This Thinking Tool

The purpose of the "Experimental Innovation Cycle" thinking tool is to systematize the process of innovation through structured experimentation in business contexts. It enables businesses to approach problem-solving with a mindset that views failures and setbacks as valuable learning opportunities. Each step in the tool is designed to guide businesses through a cycle of envisioning innovation, formulating hypotheses, designing and executing experiments, and analyzing results. This cycle not only fosters resilience and adaptability but also encourages a culture of continuous improvement and strategic growth. By iteratively refining ideas and approaches based on real-world feedback and insights, businesses can develop innovative solutions and adapt more effectively to an ever-evolving market landscape. This tool is particularly useful for businesses seeking to foster a culture of innovation and for those looking to navigate complex challenges with a methodical, data-driven approach.

## Why Use This Thinking Tool

In business problem-solving, viewing experimentation and failure as learning opportunities is vital. Each failure provides new insights, debunking assumptions and revealing unforeseen paths, much like a tech startup refining its software through testing. This approach fosters resilience and innovative thinking, transforming setbacks into strategic growth opportunities. Embracing this mindset allows businesses to adapt and thrive in an ever-changing landscape, making failures stepping stones to success.

## How To Use This Thinking Tool

```
|===========================|===============================================================================|
| Step                      |                                     Action                                    |
|===========================|===============================================================================|
| Define Innovation Goal    | Determine the specific innovation you aim to explore in the experiment.       |
| Set Learning Goals        | Define what you want to learn or understand from conducting the experiment.   |
| Formulate Hypothesis      | Formulate your expected outcome or result of the experiment.                  |
| Plan Experiment           | Outline the steps of the experiment, ensuring simplicity for effectiveness.   |
| Conduct Test & Evaluation | Compare the actual outcomes of the test with your initial hypothesis.         |
| Extract Insights          | Highlight key learnings and insights gained from the experiment.              |
| Determine Next Actions    | Decide the subsequent action or experiment, making minimal changes for speed. |
|===========================|===============================================================================|
```
