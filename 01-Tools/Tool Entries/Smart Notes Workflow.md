# Smart Notes Workflow (Ahrens F/L/P)

**One-line summary:** A three-tier note-taking workflow — Fleeting notes (transient capture), Literature notes (source-attributed), Permanent notes (atomic, linked, evergreen) — feeding writing output.

**When to reach for it:** Researchers, writers, and lifelong learners building a personal knowledge base over time; alternative to highlighting-only or unstructured note-taking.

## Purpose

Ahrens articulates Luhmann's slip-box practice as a tiered workflow. Fleeting notes capture in-the-moment thinking with no expectation of survival; Literature notes summarize sources in your own words; Permanent notes are atomic claims linked to other Permanent notes, forming an evolving network. The workflow's key insight: the writing-output emerges from re-arrangement of Permanent notes, not from blank-page composition. Many later PKM systems descend from this workflow.

## How To Use

1) Capture ideas as Fleeting notes during reading or thinking. 2) Process Fleeting into Literature notes — your own summary of a source claim. 3) Process Literature into Permanent — atomic, your own framing, linked to existing notes. 4) Build the network over time through linking. 5) Compose by re-arranging Permanent notes into argument sequences.
