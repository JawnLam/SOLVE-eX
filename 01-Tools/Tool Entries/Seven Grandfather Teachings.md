# Anishinaabe Seven Grandfather Teachings

**One-line summary:** Seven core Anishinaabe (Ojibway) values — Wisdom (nibwaakaawin), Love (zaagi'idiwin), Respect (minaadendamowin), Bravery (aakode'ewin), Honesty (gwayakwaadiziwin), Humility (dabaadendiziwin), Truth (debwewin) — as a daily orientation tool, each value with origin-narrative.

**When to reach for it:** Personal practice across cultural contexts where the teachings have been shared by Anishinaabe teachers for that purpose. Used by Anishinaabe communities; also taught by Anishinaabe teachers in cross-cultural contexts (education, healing work, leadership development).

## Purpose

Western character development often abstracts virtues from origin and community. The Seven Grandfather Teachings tie each value to a specific creation story (Mishomis tradition) and to a corresponding spirit animal — embedding character in narrative, kinship, and land. The teachings function as a daily orientation: practitioners review them, identify which they are currently working with, and notice incidents through that lens.

## How To Use

1. **Learn the seven** with their origin stories, ideally from an Anishinaabe knowledge-keeper or community context.
2. **Daily review** — at morning or evening, hold all seven; notice which is most alive today.
3. **Single-trait focus** — over a week or longer, hold one teaching at the front of awareness; observe its presence and absence in daily life.
4. **Reflection** — periodically write or share where the teaching showed up clearly and where it was missing.
5. **Cycle through all seven** — the practice is lifelong; the teachings deepen with each return.
