# Holotropic Breathwork

**One-line summary:** Stanislav and Christina Grof's protocol using intensified breathing plus evocative music plus focused bodywork to access non-ordinary states for healing and insight. Replaced LSD-assisted therapy after legal restriction.

**When to reach for it:** Therapeutic context for accessing material not reachable by ordinary cognition — birth trauma, transpersonal experience, deep psychological integration. Requires a trained facilitator and a safe container; not a self-administered practice.

## Purpose

Stanislav Grof's research with LSD-assisted therapy in the 1960s mapped a phenomenology of non-ordinary states (perinatal, biographical, transpersonal). When LSD became illegal, the Grofs developed Holotropic Breathwork as a drug-free analogue: hyperventilation plus music plus bodywork induces a similar state. The state itself is healing — material that needs integration surfaces and resolves without verbal-cognitive intervention.

## How To Use

1. **Workshop setup** — typically a weekend or week-long retreat with trained facilitators. Pairs of "breather" and "sitter."
2. **Breather phase** (3 hours) — deep, fast, continuous breathing while lying down, eyes closed, listening to curated music (specific arc — driving, breakthrough, heart-opening, integrative).
3. **Sitter supports** — present but non-intervening unless the breather requests bodywork or assistance.
4. **Bodywork (focused energy release)** — facilitator helps the breather work through stuck physical-emotional material.
5. **Mandala drawing** — wordless integration through art.
6. **Group sharing** — verbal integration the following day.
