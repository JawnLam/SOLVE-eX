# Fixes That Fail (Senge Archetype #3)

**One-line summary:** A fix that addresses the immediate symptom generates delayed unintended consequences that recreate or worsen the original problem.

**When to reach for it:** Policy interventions with downstream consequences; antibiotic over-prescription; pesticide cycles; deficit-spending dynamics; growth-by-acquisition strategies.

## Purpose

Many fixes work in the short term but generate delayed consequences that re-create the original problem, often worse. The delay disguises the cause: by the time the consequence manifests, the original fix is decoupled from it in memory. The pattern recurs because the immediate relief is felt while the delayed consequence is not yet visible. The diagnostic is asking explicitly: 'what does this fix do over the long term, after the system has absorbed it?'

## How To Use

1) Identify the fix and the problem it addresses. 2) Trace the consequences of the fix beyond its immediate effect. 3) Identify delayed feedbacks that may recreate the problem. 4) If found, the fix is high-risk for long-term failure. 5) Look for alternative interventions that don't generate the same delayed feedback. 6) When uncertain, pilot-test and monitor delayed feedback signals.
