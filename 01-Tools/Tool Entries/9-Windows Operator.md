# 9-Windows / Multi-Screen Operator

**One-line summary:** A 3 x 3 matrix — past / present / future on one axis, subsystem / system / supersystem on the other — used to expand context before problem-solving.

**When to reach for it:** Defining the system boundary for problem-solving; foresight workshops; ensuring that solutions don't optimize the system at the cost of supersystem or subsystem.

## Purpose

Problem-solvers default to the current system at its current time, missing constraints and opportunities at adjacent scales and times. The 9-Windows operator forces explicit consideration of all nine cells: what was the system in the past? what is the supersystem in the future? etc. Innovative solutions often come from cells that conventional analysis skips — e.g., resources in the supersystem, or analogues from the past.

## How To Use

1) Draw the 3 x 3 grid. 2) Fill the center (current system, present). 3) Fill the row: subsystem, system, supersystem. 4) Fill the column: past, present, future. 5) Complete all 9 cells. 6) Identify opportunities or constraints from the previously-skipped cells. 7) Use as input to ARIZ or other problem-solving.
