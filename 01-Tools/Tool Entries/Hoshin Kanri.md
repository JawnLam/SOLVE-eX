# Hoshin Kanri / Policy Deployment

**One-line summary:** A Japanese strategic-planning method — cascade a small number of breakthrough objectives from top leadership through every level of the organization via catchball dialogue.

**When to reach for it:** Strategic alignment in larger organizations; ensuring that frontline activity actually serves strategy; Lean-aligned organizations adopting strategic deployment.

## Purpose

Hoshin Kanri (translated as 'policy deployment') is Toyota's strategic-planning method. The method combines top-down direction (leadership sets a small number of breakthrough objectives) with bottom-up dialogue (each level negotiates with the level above on how to deliver — 'catchball'). The result is alignment that connects strategic intent to frontline activity without mere cascading. The X-matrix is a common visualization tool. The method is more demanding than typical strategic planning but produces deeper alignment.

## How To Use

1) Top leadership identifies 3-5 breakthrough objectives for the year. 2) Cascade objectives through levels; each level translates to its own contribution. 3) Use catchball dialogue: lower levels push back on what's feasible; upper levels adjust or hold the line. 4) Document with X-matrix or similar visualization. 5) Monitor monthly; adjust as needed. 6) Annual cycle.
