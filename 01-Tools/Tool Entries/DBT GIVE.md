# DBT GIVE

**One-line summary:** Mnemonic for relationship-preserving communication — be Gentle, act Interested, Validate, use Easy manner.

**When to reach for it:** Conversations where the relationship matters as much as the outcome; navigating disagreements without escalation; clients working on relationship effectiveness.

## Purpose

DEAR MAN focuses on the task (getting what you want); GIVE focuses on the relationship (preserving the connection). The two are typically combined — a request made via DEAR MAN executed with GIVE preserves both. GIVE emphasizes the affective texture: gentleness, genuine interest, explicit validation of the other's perspective, and an easy (warm, non-rigid) manner. Skipping GIVE often produces tactically successful requests that damage the relationship over time.

## How To Use

1) Gentle — no attacks, threats, or judgments. 2) Interested — actually listen to the other's response. 3) Validate — explicitly acknowledge the other's feelings and viewpoint. 4) Easy manner — light tone, no rigidity, even some humor when appropriate.
