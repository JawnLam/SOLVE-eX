# IFS Parts Mapping

**One-line summary:** A cartographic exercise inventorying internal parts and their relationships — typically Managers (protective), Firefighters (reactive protective), and Exiles (carrying burdens).

**When to reach for it:** Early IFS work to map the internal system; therapy intake; coaching contexts where the client benefits from seeing their internal structure laid out.

## Purpose

IFS posits the mind as a system of parts. Parts Mapping inventories them: which parts show up frequently? Which protect which? Which are in conflict? The map makes the internal structure visible — reducing the felt sense of being undifferentiated and overwhelmed. The cartographic move (paper-and-pen, or sand tray, or art) externalizes parts, making them objects of curious inspection rather than fused-with experience.

## How To Use

1) Use paper, sand tray, or art materials. 2) Ask: what parts of me show up regularly? 3) Note each part's function (manager, firefighter, exile per IFS typology). 4) Draw relationships: which protects which? which conflicts with which? 5) Note Self's relationship to each. 6) Use as map for ongoing IFS work.
