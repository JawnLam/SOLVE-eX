# Escalation (Senge Archetype #7)

**One-line summary:** Two parties each respond to perceived threats with counter-measures; each counter-measure raises the threat to the other; the system spirals upward.

**When to reach for it:** Arms races; price wars; advertising competition; biological co-evolution; any reciprocal-threat dynamic.

## Purpose

Escalation is the reciprocal pattern: A's action raises perceived threat for B; B's response raises perceived threat for A; the system ratchets upward indefinitely. Each step is locally rational from the actor's perspective but globally destructive. The same logic generates arms races, advertising-budget wars, and many trade disputes. The diagnostic is recognizing reciprocal-threat structure; the intervention is unilateral de-escalation, third-party mediation, or binding constraint.

## How To Use

1) Map the reciprocal-threat structure: A's actions, B's perceived threat, B's response, A's perceived threat. 2) Confirm the system is escalating, not stable. 3) Choose intervention: unilateral de-escalation (risky but breaks cycle), binding agreement (requires trust or third-party enforcement), shared external constraint. 4) Communicate clearly to avoid mis-interpretation of de-escalation as weakness.
