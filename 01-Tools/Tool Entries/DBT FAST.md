# DBT FAST

**One-line summary:** Mnemonic for self-respect-preserving communication — be Fair, Apologies-not (no over-apologizing), Stick to values, Truthful.

**When to reach for it:** Difficult conversations where the temptation is to capitulate or over-apologize; coaching clients with passive or fawning patterns; protecting integrity in negotiation.

## Purpose

DEAR MAN handles the task; GIVE handles the relationship; FAST handles self-respect. The three are typically combined when all three matter. FAST reminds the speaker not to give up integrity for compliance: don't be unfair to yourself; don't over-apologize when not at fault; stick to your values even under pressure; tell the truth (don't make excuses or exaggerate). The mnemonic counteracts the conflict-avoidant collapse pattern.

## How To Use

1) Fair — be fair to yourself and the other. 2) Apologies-not — don't apologize unless you've actually done something wrong. 3) Stick to values — don't compromise core values for short-term peace. 4) Truthful — don't lie, exaggerate, or make excuses.
