# Neti-Neti (Vedantic Negation Method)

**One-line summary:** Vedantic apophatic method from the Brihadaranyaka Upanishad. For any candidate description of Brahman/Self, negate it: "not this, not that." Continues until conceptual mind exhausts grasping.

**When to reach for it:** Within Vedantic contemplative inquiry, especially as preparation for Atma Vichara or as a method for releasing identifications with body, mind, role, history. Useful for any inquiry where every positive characterization is a partial truth that occludes the whole.

## Purpose

The Upanishadic insight: Brahman / the ultimate / the true Self cannot be characterized by any positive description, because every description names a feature that contrasts with its absence — and Brahman has no contrast. Whatever you can describe is therefore not it. *Neti, neti* — not this, not that. The method is not nihilistic; it is liberating: by systematically dropping every misidentification, the practitioner releases the obstructions to direct realization.

## How To Use

Working with the question "Who am I?" or "What is the true Self?":
1. **Body?** No — the Self witnesses the body. Neti.
2. **Thoughts?** No — the Self witnesses thoughts. Neti.
3. **Feelings?** No — the Self witnesses feelings. Neti.
4. **Role / history / personality?** No — these change; the Self is what remains constant. Neti.
5. **Awareness?** Notice the move to grasp awareness as the Self. Awareness is what remains, but to *name* it is to grasp again. Neti.
6. **Rest** in what remains when every characterization is released.
7. **Repeat** — daily; over time, identifications loosen and direct knowing arises.
