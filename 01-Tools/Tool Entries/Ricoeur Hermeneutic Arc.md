# Ricoeur Hermeneutic Arc

**One-line summary:** A three-phase interpretation movement — initial naive understanding, then structural/explanatory analysis (distanciation), then deeper appropriated understanding.

**When to reach for it:** Interpretation tasks that require moving beyond first impressions through explicit analytic distance back to deeper integration; teaching critical reading.

## Purpose

Ricoeur synthesized hermeneutics and structuralism via the arc: the reader's first encounter with a text is naive understanding; structural analysis distances the reader from that initial reading and reveals patterns, contradictions, and constraints; the third movement re-integrates the analyzed text into a deeper understanding that is informed by both naïveté and analysis. The arc captures a methodological insight common across critical-reading traditions: appropriation requires distanciation.

## How To Use

1) First reading: notice initial impressions, surface meaning, immediate reactions. 2) Distanciation: apply structural / analytic methods (formalist analysis, historical critique, ideological analysis). 3) Notice patterns invisible to naive reading. 4) Re-read: integrate analytic findings into a richer understanding. 5) The end-state is not the initial reading nor the analysis but a synthesis that respects both.
