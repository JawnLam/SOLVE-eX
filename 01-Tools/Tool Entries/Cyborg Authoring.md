# Cyborg Authoring

**One-line summary:** Iterative co-writing protocol where human and AI alternate generation, critique, and refinement at fine granularity. Distinct from "AI-as-tool" pattern where the human prompts and the AI completes.

**When to reach for it:** Producing written or structured artifacts (essays, briefs, analyses, code, plans) where the human's judgment about quality, voice, or accuracy is the gating constraint and the AI's generation speed is the bottleneck-reliever. Most useful when neither pure-human nor pure-AI produces the artifact quality the task demands.

## Purpose

Mollick distinguishes Centaur (human steers, AI generates blocks) from Cyborg (deeper interleaving — AI handles sub-tasks transparently within a single workflow, with the human shifting fluidly between authoring and editing). The Cyborg pattern produces higher-quality artifacts but requires the human to maintain context-switching capacity across many micro-interactions.

## How To Use

1. **Decompose the artifact** into chunks the AI can produce credibly (research summary, draft section, code snippet, edit suggestion).
2. **Author the spine** — outline, key claims, voice samples — yourself.
3. **Generate each chunk via AI** with explicit constraints (length, voice, source list).
4. **Read AI output as a draft** — accept what works, reject or rewrite what doesn't.
5. **Critique loops** — feed your edits back to the AI for next-pass refinement.
6. **Final pass entirely human** — voice continuity, fact-checking, signature decisions.

The boundary between Centaur and Cyborg is the depth of interleaving. Cyborg means many small handoffs; Centaur means fewer, larger ones.
