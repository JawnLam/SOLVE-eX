# Forum Theatre (Boal)

**One-line summary:** Augusto Boal's Theatre-of-the-Oppressed protocol where audience members ("spect-actors") interrupt a played-out scene of oppression and substitute themselves to try alternative responses, mediated by a "Joker" facilitator.

**When to reach for it:** Community-based exploration of oppression and power dynamics — workplace dynamics, social conflicts, structural injustice. Used heavily in social-change work, education, conflict transformation. Effective where abstract analysis fails to generate lived insight.

## Purpose

Boal's insight: oppression rehearses one script (the oppressed cannot win). Forum Theatre breaks the script by inviting audience members to step onto the stage and try alternative responses. The audience becomes "spect-actors" rather than spectators. The accumulated experiments — usually 6-12 alternative responses from different spect-actors — surface a repertoire of possibilities the original script foreclosed. The Joker (facilitator) keeps interventions productive without dictating answers.

## How To Use

1. **Develop the scene** — Workshop with a community to create a 5-10 minute scene depicting an oppression they face. Script ends with the oppressed protagonist's defeat.
2. **Play the scene through** — Audience watches.
3. **Replay with interventions** — Joker invites audience to call "stop" at any point and replace the protagonist to try an alternative response.
4. **Reality test** — The other actors stay in character; if the intervention is unrealistic, it fails (the oppression continues). Realistic interventions are explored.
5. **Debrief** — What worked? What didn't? What patterns emerge? Joker doesn't conclude — the community does.
