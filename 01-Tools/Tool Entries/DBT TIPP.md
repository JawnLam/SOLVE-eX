# DBT TIPP (Distress Tolerance)

**One-line summary:** A multi-modal crisis-regulation protocol — Temperature (cold water), Intense exercise, Paced breathing, Paired muscle relaxation.

**When to reach for it:** Acute emotional dysregulation; suicidal or self-harm urges; panic attacks; moments where thinking-based coping fails because the nervous system is overactivated.

## Purpose

When the nervous system is highly activated, cognitive coping (thinking it through) doesn't work — the prefrontal cortex is offline. TIPP targets the parasympathetic system through physiological interventions: cold water to the face (dive reflex) drops heart rate within seconds; intense exercise depletes activation; paced breathing (exhale longer than inhale) signals safety; paired muscle relaxation reduces somatic tension. The protocol is teachable, portable, and effective in minutes.

## How To Use

1) Cold: splash face with cold water or hold ice pack to eyes/face for 15-30 seconds. 2) Intense Exercise: run, jump, do burpees for ~10-15 minutes. 3) Paced Breathing: inhale 4, exhale 6-8, for 5-10 minutes. 4) Paired Muscle Relaxation: tense and release muscle groups while exhaling. Use any one or all four.
