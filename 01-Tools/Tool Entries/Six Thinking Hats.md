# Six Thinking Hats

**One-line summary:** A parallel-thinking protocol where the group rotates through six explicit perspective-modes — White (data), Red (emotion), Black (caution), Yellow (benefit), Green (creativity), Blue (process) — one at a time.

**When to reach for it:** Group decisions where stakeholders default to argument rather than analysis; ideation that needs both divergent and critical phases; teaching meta-cognition about perspective rotation.

## Purpose

Adversarial group discussion has people simultaneously holding different perspectives (one critiquing, one defending, one optimistic, one anxious), producing confusion and ego defense. Six Hats serializes the perspectives: everyone is critical at the same time (Black), everyone is generative at the same time (Green), and so on. The result is more parallel exploration, less argumentation, and explicit visiting of perspectives that might otherwise be skipped.

## How To Use

1) Frame the question. 2) Choose a sequence of hats. 3) For each hat, everyone thinks in that mode for a fixed time. 4) Capture outputs. 5) Continue through all relevant hats. 6) Blue hat synthesizes process and next steps.
