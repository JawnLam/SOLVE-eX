# Ifa Divination System

**One-line summary:** Yoruba (West African) divination system using the opele chain or 16 sacred kola/palm nuts on the Opon Ifa tray. Mathematical permutation yields one of 256 odu (signs), each linked to verses of wisdom (ese ifa) consulted by the babalawo for the seeker. UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage.

**When to reach for it:** Within Yoruba religious practice and the African diaspora traditions (Lukumí, Candomblé, etc.) where Ifa is a primary discernment system. Engaged with through trained babalawo / iyanifa; not extracted to outside practice without permission and apprenticeship.

## Purpose

Ifa is the Yoruba people's encoded body of cultural wisdom in the form of 256 *odu* — each odu is a structural sign with associated verses (*ese*), stories, and practical guidance. When a seeker comes with a question, the babalawo casts the opele or palm nuts, generating a specific odu pattern; the babalawo then recites the relevant verses and interprets them in light of the seeker's situation. The system is simultaneously: a divinatory practice, a corpus of philosophical/ethical literature, a mathematical-symbolic system (16² = 256 binary-coded signs), and a cosmological framework.

## How To Use

Ifa consultation requires a trained babalawo (or iyanifa). Structural outline (for understanding, not extraction):
1. **Seeker brings a question** to the babalawo.
2. **Casting** — Opele chain (or 16 kola nuts) is thrown; the configuration determines the odu.
3. **Identify the odu** — One of 256, named (Eji Ogbe, Oyeku Meji, etc.).
4. **Recite ese** — Babalawo recites memorized verses associated with the odu.
5. **Interpret in context** — Verses are interpreted in light of the seeker's specific question.
6. **Prescribe** — Often includes a *ebo* (offering or action) the seeker is to perform.

The corpus is vast; mastery requires years of apprenticeship.
