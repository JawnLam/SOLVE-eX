# Evolutionary Reasoning

**One-line summary:** Whenever variation + selection + retention is operative, evolutionary dynamics apply — to ideas, technologies, firms, cultures, not just biological species.

**When to reach for it:** Analyzing the long-term success of ideas/products/practices; understanding why intuitive 'designed' explanations fail; cultural evolution and technology adoption analysis.

## Purpose

The Darwinian three-step (variation, selection, retention) is substrate-independent — wherever it operates, complexity can accumulate without a designer. The reasoning move is recognizing the pattern in non-biological domains: scientific ideas evolve under peer-review selection, technologies evolve under market selection, organizations evolve under survival selection. The implication: 'why does it look designed?' is often answered by 'because evolution shaped it under operative selection pressure,' not by intentional design.

## How To Use

1) Ask: is there variation in the population (of ideas, products, firms)? 2) Is there differential selection (some survive, some don't)? 3) Is there retention (successful variants persist)? 4) If yes to all three, evolutionary dynamics are operative. 5) Apply evolutionary intuitions: convergent traits suggest selection pressure; vestigial features suggest historical selection that no longer applies; arms-race dynamics suggest reciprocal selection.
