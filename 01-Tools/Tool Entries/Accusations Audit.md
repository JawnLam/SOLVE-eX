# Accusations Audit (Voss)

**One-line summary:** Pre-emptively name and acknowledge every negative accusation the counterparty might level — disarms objections by stating them first.

**When to reach for it:** Difficult conversations where the counterparty is likely to raise specific accusations or concerns; sales pitches under skepticism; performance conversations.

## Purpose

Counterparties have unstated concerns and accusations that, if voiced first by them, become entrenched in the conversation. The Accusations Audit pre-empts this: list every negative thing they might think or say, then acknowledge each before they do. 'You probably think I'm coming to ask for too much.' 'You might be skeptical because of [prior event].' The technique disarms the objections by removing their air supply — the counterparty can't fight against acknowledgement. Voss describes it as one of the most powerful negotiation techniques.

## How To Use

1) Before the difficult conversation, list every negative thing the counterparty might think about you or your position. 2) Phrase each as an acknowledgement: 'You might think...', 'It might seem...', 'You probably feel...' 3) Voice them early in the conversation, before they do. 4) Notice the shift — counterparty is no longer holding the accusations defensively.
