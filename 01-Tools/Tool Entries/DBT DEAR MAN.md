# DBT DEAR MAN

**One-line summary:** A structured assertive-request mnemonic — Describe, Express, Assert, Reinforce; Mindful, Appear confident, Negotiate.

**When to reach for it:** Making a specific request of another person where being clear and effective matters; coaching clients with assertion difficulties; structuring difficult conversations.

## Purpose

Many requests fail because they're indirect, emotionally loaded, or muddled. DEAR MAN structures the request into clear stages: describe the situation factually, express your feelings about it, assert what you want, reinforce why it benefits the other. MAN reminds the speaker to stay Mindful of the goal, Appear confident in tone, Negotiate where flexible. The mnemonic is teachable in one session and improves request outcomes measurably.

## How To Use

1) Describe: state the facts (no interpretation). 2) Express: share your feelings or thoughts. 3) Assert: state your specific request directly. 4) Reinforce: state the benefit of compliance. 5) Stay Mindful (don't get derailed by side issues). 6) Appear confident (tone, posture, eye contact). 7) Negotiate (offer alternatives if needed).
