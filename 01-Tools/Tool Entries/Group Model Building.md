# Group Model Building (Vennix)

**One-line summary:** A facilitated workshop method for collaboratively building system-dynamics models with stakeholders — produces both a model and shared understanding.

**When to reach for it:** Multi-stakeholder strategic problems where the system structure is contested; organizational learning interventions; policy modeling with affected parties at the table.

## Purpose

Expert-built system-dynamics models often fail in implementation because the stakeholders who must act on them don't share the analysts' mental model. Group Model Building puts the stakeholders in the modeling process: structured workshop steps elicit variables, polarity, loops, and policy levers from the group. The resulting model is both technically credible and politically owned. The discipline is the structured facilitation that keeps the group productive while building a working model.

## How To Use

1) Pre-workshop: scope the question, identify stakeholders, prepare scripts. 2) Workshop: elicit variables; identify reinforcing and balancing loops; identify reference modes; simulate together. 3) Iterate over multiple sessions if needed. 4) Use the shared model to design policy. 5) Document for downstream implementation.
