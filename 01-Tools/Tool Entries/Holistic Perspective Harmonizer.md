# Holistic Perspective Harmonizer

**One-line summary:** Owner / empathy / external / contrary / unbiased viewpoints → consolidated problem statement.

**When to reach for it:** You suspect you're seeing the situation from too narrow an angle; need stakeholder perspectives surfaced.

---

## Purpose Of This Thinking Tool

The purpose of the "Holistic Perspective Harmonizer" thinking tool is to enrich the understanding of a situation by embracing the diversity of viewpoints. Recognizing that individuals may see problems and opportunities through different lenses, this tool seeks to compile these varied perspectives to form a more complete picture. By doing so, it aims to facilitate a more effective and empathetic problem-solving approach.

The "Holistic Perspective Harmonizer" is used by actively soliciting the views and experiences of different stakeholders involved or affected by the issue at hand. This includes not only those responsible for resolving the problem but also those who experience its impact, those who are distant from the situation, and even those who may disagree with the prevailing opinion. By considering these different angles, biases can be identified and mitigated, allowing for a more objective reevaluation of the issue.

Ultimately, this tool helps to distill a wide array of perceptions into a singular, coherent problem statement that accurately reflects the complexities of the real-world scenario. It encourages inclusive dialogue and deeper analysis, leading to solutions that are considerate of all angles and implications.

## Why Use This Thinking Tool

Issues and chances are seen differently by different people. What seems like a problem or chance to one person might not seem the same to someone else. It's important to try and see things from other people's perspectives to understand a situation better.

## How To Use This Thinking Tool

Use this method to gather various opinions on a topic. Feel free to include as many different viewpoints as necessary.

## The Actual Thinking Tool

```
|==============================|=====================================================================================|=================|
|          Viewpoint           |                                    Instruction                                      | User Entry Here |
|==============================|=====================================================================================|=================|
| Original Problem/Opportunity | Restate the Original Problem, Opportunity, or Situation                             |                 |
| Owner's Perspective          | Describe the issue from the viewpoint of the person tasked with solving it.         |                 |
| Empathy Perspective          | Recast the issue from the perspective of others directly affected by it.            |                 |
| External Perspective         | Guess how those who aren't closely involved might perceive the issue.               |                 |
| Contrary Perspective         | Express the issue from the standpoint of someone who has the opposite opinion.      |                 |
| Unbiased Perspective         | Recognize and set aside your biases, then redefine the issue from a neutral stance. |                 |
| Additional Perspectives      | Include any other relevant viewpoints that could shed more light on the issue.      |                 |
|==============================|=====================================================================================|=================|
| Final Problem Statement      | Consolidate all perspectives into a comprehensive problem or opportunity statement. |                 |
|==============================|=====================================================================================|=================|
```
