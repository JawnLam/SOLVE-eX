# Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs

**One-line summary:** A staged hierarchy of human needs — physiological / safety / belonging / esteem / self-actualization — where lower-stage deficits dominate motivation until met.

**When to reach for it:** Diagnosing motivation gaps in workplaces, designing for users at different stages, understanding why higher-order interventions fail when basic needs are unmet.

## Purpose

Maslow's claim is descriptive (humans appear to prioritize unmet lower-stage needs) and diagnostic (a person stuck at safety-level concerns will not respond to self-actualization interventions). The model is widely critiqued (the pyramid shape is post-hoc, the strict staging is contested, cross-cultural applicability is uneven) but persists as a practical first-pass framework for asking 'what level of need is currently underdetermined?'

## How To Use

1) For an individual or population, identify which stage's needs are currently met. 2) For ones that aren't, focus interventions there — higher-stage offerings will be ignored. 3) Recognize that the stages aren't strictly sequential in practice — needs interact and recur. 4) Treat as a heuristic, not a law.
