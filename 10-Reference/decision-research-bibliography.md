# Decision Research — Bibliography

This bibliography lists the foundational works behind the SOLVE eX
decision-making model and the library's tool entries. Citations are
grouped by domain. Tool library entries that derive from a cited work
are linked by canonical title.

This file is a starting-point for readers who want to go upstream of
the library — not an exhaustive academic bibliography. Where a tool's
library entry has its own bibliography section, that entry is the
canonical source for that tool's references.

---

## 1. Decision theory (analytic / formal)

- **Edwards, W. (1954).** "The Theory of Decision Making." *Psychological
  Bulletin*, 51(4), 380–417. Foundational synthesis of expected-utility
  theory and its experimental tests. Background for the library's
  Expected Value Decision Trees and Weighted Decision Matrix entries.
- **Raiffa, H. (1968).** *Decision Analysis: Introductory Lectures on
  Choices under Uncertainty*. Addison-Wesley. The canonical reference
  for decision-tree analysis. Underpins library tools: Expected Value
  Decision Trees, Settlement Decision Tree.
- **Savage, L. J. (1954).** *The Foundations of Statistics*. Wiley.
  Subjective expected utility framework. Background for the library's
  Bayesian Updating entry.
- **Saaty, T. L. (1980).** *The Analytic Hierarchy Process*. McGraw-Hill.
  Pairwise comparison for criterion weighting. Underpins library tool:
  AHP.

## 2. Behavioral decision research

- **Kahneman, D., & Tversky, A. (1979).** "Prospect Theory: An Analysis
  of Decision under Risk." *Econometrica*, 47(2), 263–292. Foundational
  paper for understanding how actual decision-making diverges from
  expected-utility predictions. Background for many library tools that
  surface cognitive biases.
- **Kahneman, D. (2011).** *Thinking, Fast and Slow*. Farrar, Straus
  and Giroux. The integrated synthesis. Background reading for any
  diagnostic work in the SOLVE eX model.
- **Gigerenzer, G., & Selten, R. (eds.) (2001).** *Bounded Rationality:
  The Adaptive Toolbox*. MIT Press. The case for ecological rationality
  and heuristic reasoning. Background for library heuristic tools
  (Bradford Hill Criteria, the Two-Minute Rule).
- **Hastie, R., & Dawes, R. M. (2010).** *Rational Choice in an
  Uncertain World*, 2nd ed. SAGE. Pedagogically clearest text on the
  intersection of normative and descriptive decision theory.

## 3. Strategy and applied decision-making

- **Porter, M. E. (1980).** *Competitive Strategy*. Free Press. Five-
  forces framework; underpins library strategic-analysis tools.
- **Younger, S. (2024).** *Younger's Commandments* [public collection].
  Negative-heuristic compilation for business strategy. Library tool:
  Younger Ten Commandments.
- **Kim, W. C., & Mauborgne, R. (2005).** *Blue Ocean Strategy*. HBS
  Press. Library tool: Blue Ocean Strategy Canvas.
- **Wilkinson, A. (1995–present).** *Scenario Planning: A Tool for
  Strategic Thinking*. Shell scenario-planning canon. Library tool:
  Scenario Planning.

## 4. Meaning-making and narrative

- **Chochinov, H. M. (2012).** *Dignity Therapy: Final Words for Final
  Days*. Oxford University Press. Library tool: Dignity Therapy.
- **White, M., & Epston, D. (1990).** *Narrative Means to Therapeutic
  Ends*. W. W. Norton. Library tool: Narrative Therapy.
- **Charon, R. (2006).** *Narrative Medicine: Honoring the Stories of
  Illness*. Oxford University Press. Library tool: Narrative Medicine.
- **Frankl, V. E. (1959).** *Man's Search for Meaning*. Background for
  library tool: Frankl Logotherapy.
- **Campbell, J. (1949).** *The Hero with a Thousand Faces*. Pantheon.
  Background for library tool: Heros Journey.

## 5. Systems thinking and feedback

- **Senge, P. M. (1990).** *The Fifth Discipline*. Doubleday/Currency.
  Systems-thinking introduction; underpins library tools using causal-
  loop framing.
- **Meadows, D. H. (2008).** *Thinking in Systems*. Chelsea Green.
  Operational systems-thinking text. Library tools: Causal Loop
  Diagrams, archetype-named feedback patterns.
- **Sterman, J. (2000).** *Business Dynamics: Systems Thinking and
  Modeling for a Complex World*. McGraw-Hill. The graduate-level text
  for system dynamics.

## 6. Contemplative traditions

- **Suzuki, S. (1970).** *Zen Mind, Beginner's Mind*. Weatherhill.
  Library tool: Beginner's Mind.
- **Lao Tzu (~ 6th century BCE).** *Tao Te Ching*. Multiple translations
  (Mitchell, Le Guin, Stephen Addiss). Library tool: Wu Wei Decision
  Practice.
- **Maharshi, R. (1902–1950).** *Who Am I?* (collected dialogues).
  Library tool: Atma Vichara.
- **Yokoyama, R. (1972).** "Naikan in Japanese Mental Health." Library
  tool: Naikan.

## 7. Stakeholder and relational decision-making

- **Mendelow, A. L. (1981).** "Environmental Scanning — The Impact of
  the Stakeholder Concept." Foundation for the Stakeholder Power-
  Interest Grid. Library tool: Stakeholder Power-Interest Grid.
- **Mitchell, R. K., Agle, B. R., & Wood, D. J. (1997).** "Toward a
  Theory of Stakeholder Identification and Salience." *Academy of
  Management Review*, 22(4), 853–886. Foundational stakeholder-theory
  paper.

## 8. Therapy / counseling traditions referenced

- **Beck, A. T. (1976).** *Cognitive Therapy and the Emotional
  Disorders*. International Universities Press. Background for
  cognitive-restructuring library tools.
- **Rosenberg, M. B. (2003).** *Nonviolent Communication: A Language of
  Life*. PuddleDancer Press. Library tools: NVC Self-Empathy Practice,
  Tactical Empathy (related).
- **Linehan, M. M. (1993).** *Cognitive-Behavioral Treatment of
  Borderline Personality Disorder*. Guilford. Background for library
  tools rooted in DBT (DBT FAST).
- **Hayes, S. C., Strosahl, K. D., & Wilson, K. G. (2011).** *Acceptance
  and Commitment Therapy*, 2nd ed. Guilford. Library tool: ACT Values
  Clarification.

## 9. Game theory and strategic interaction

- **von Neumann, J., & Morgenstern, O. (1944).** *Theory of Games and
  Economic Behavior*. Princeton University Press. Foundational.
- **Schelling, T. (1960).** *The Strategy of Conflict*. Harvard
  University Press. Operational game-theoretic strategy. Background
  for library tools: Bayesian Games, Signaling Games.
- **Axelrod, R. (1984).** *The Evolution of Cooperation*. Basic Books.
  Library tool: Repeated Games Folk Theorem.

## 10. Practical philosophy and ethics

- **Aristotle (~ 350 BCE).** *Nicomachean Ethics*. Foundational text on
  practical wisdom (`tt_Domain: Phronetic / practical wisdom`).
- **Epictetus (~ 125 CE).** *Discourses* and *Enchiridion*. Stoic
  philosophy underpinning library equanimity tools.
- **Aurelius, M. (~ 170 CE).** *Meditations*. Stoic practice.
- **Foot, P. (2001).** *Natural Goodness*. Oxford University Press.
  Philosophical grounding for cross-persona principle of user values
  autonomy.

## 11. Operations and after-action methods

- **US Army (various editions).** *After Action Review Handbook*.
  Library tool: After Action Review.
- **Sutton, R. I., & Rao, H. (2014).** *Scaling Up Excellence*.
  Crown Business. Background for the library's operational scaling
  tools.

## 12. Foundational works on practical ethics in helping professions

- **Schön, D. A. (1983).** *The Reflective Practitioner*. Basic Books.
  Library tool: Schon Reflection. Background for the meta-conversation
  pattern (chapter 11).

## How to use this bibliography

This file is a pointer, not a primary source. For any tool the library
ships, the tool's own entry in `01-Tools/Tool Entries/` is the
authoritative reference — including its application pattern, its
canonical title, and any tool-specific bibliography section. Use this
file when:

- You want to read the foundational source behind a library tool.
- You are extending the corpus and need to ground a new tool in the
  literature.
- You are writing about the SOLVE eX model and need to cite the
  decision-research lineage it draws from.

For a working AI in session, this file is not load-bearing. The library
tools and the operating manual chapters carry the operational content;
this file carries the lineage.
