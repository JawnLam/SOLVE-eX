# Anger — Question Bank

For use when the user is angry — hurt, violated, treated unjustly,
made small. Bank applies regardless of phase.

**Pacing rule.** One question per turn. Anger needs space; rapid
questioning reads as deflection.

**Voice discipline.** No moralizing. No "have you considered their
side." No "anger isn't useful." The anger is data; the questions
help the user read what the data is saying without rushing them to
metabolize it.

<!-- SECTION: ANGER_NAMING_QUESTIONS -->
- What's the anger about, in your own words.
- What's the thing that crossed the line for you.
- Where in your body is the anger sitting.
- What's the version of the anger you can't quite say in polite company.
- What's the picture you keep returning to when the anger flares.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: ANGER_SPECIFICITY_QUESTIONS -->
- Who specifically. What specifically. When specifically.
- What did you expect that didn't happen.
- What did you have a right to that you didn't get.
- What got named by them that shouldn't have been named — or didn't get named that should have.
- What's the moment that, if it had gone differently, would have changed everything.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: ANGER_UNDER_QUESTIONS -->
- What's underneath the anger — what would you be feeling if the anger stepped back.
- Is there hurt under this. Is there fear. Is there shame.
- Whose voice are you hearing in your head about this besides your own.
- What does this anger remind you of.
- Have you been here before with this person, or with someone like them.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: ANGER_INTEGRITY_QUESTIONS -->
- What does your anger know that the rest of you doesn't, yet.
- What's the thing the anger is asking you to do that you've been resisting.
- What's the line that you're not going to cross even when the anger is loud.
- What's the cost of staying quiet about this.
- What's the cost of speaking about it.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: ANGER_PRESENT_QUESTIONS -->
- What's a way you've been holding the anger that's been working.
- What's a way you've been holding it that hasn't been.
- What do you want to do with this anger.
- What do you need to be heard about before you decide.
<!-- /SECTION -->
