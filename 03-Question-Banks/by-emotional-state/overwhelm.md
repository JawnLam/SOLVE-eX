# Overwhelm — Question Bank

For use when the user is overwhelmed — too many things, too much
weight, system at or past capacity. Bank applies regardless of
phase.

**Pacing rule.** One question per turn. Multi-question turns are
contraindicated in overwhelm; the user is already at capacity.

**Voice discipline.** No solution-shaped questions yet. The work
here is naming the load before sorting it. Premature sorting reads
as more pressure.

<!-- SECTION: OVERWHELM_NAMING_QUESTIONS -->
- What's the weight of it right now, in one word.
- What's filling up most of the space.
- If you had to point at where in the body this lives, where would you point.
- What's the moment today that hit you hardest.
- What's the part of this that you've been keeping out of your own awareness.
- Where in your week did this start to crest.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: OVERWHELM_INVENTORY_QUESTIONS -->
- What are the three or four things that are most on your plate right now.
- Of those, which one is using the most cognitive space.
- Of those, which one is using the most emotional space.
- Are the cognitively-heavy ones the same as the emotionally-heavy ones, or different.
- What's something that's on the list that someone else thinks is bigger than you do.
- What's something that's on the list that you think is bigger than anyone else realizes.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: OVERWHELM_BOUNDARY_QUESTIONS -->
- What have you said yes to that you'd say no to if you could redo it.
- What's the thing you're carrying that isn't actually yours.
- Where in the inventory is there something you could put down.
- Who else is involved in any of these that hasn't been carrying their share.
- What's the smallest item on the list — the thing that's smallest but you still haven't done.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: OVERWHELM_BODY_QUESTIONS -->
- When did you last sleep through the night.
- When did you last eat without thinking about something else.
- What's the body doing right now that's a tell about overwhelm.
- Where in the day is your worst hour.
- Where in the day is your best hour.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: OVERWHELM_PERMISSION_QUESTIONS -->
- Have you told anyone the full shape of what's on your plate.
- Are you treating this as a permanent state or a temporary one.
- What's the thing you'd want someone to say to you right now.
<!-- /SECTION -->
