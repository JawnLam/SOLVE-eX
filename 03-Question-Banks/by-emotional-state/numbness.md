# Numbness — Question Bank

For use when the user is numb — flat, distant from their own
content, dissociated, "fine but not really," the affect-channel
muted. Bank applies regardless of phase.

**Pacing rule.** One question per turn. Slow pacing. Numbness has
its own valid logic; pushing for feeling reads as another demand.

**Voice discipline — load-bearing.** Numbness banks must not push
toward feeling. The numbness is a regulatory move — often a
necessary one, often after extended pressure. The questions honor
the numbness as data without demanding the user exit it.

The work is not to break the numbness open. The work is to register
what the numbness is doing, what it's protecting, when it might
soften on its own, and whether there are conditions making the
numbness harder to come back from.

<!-- SECTION: NUMBNESS_NAMING_QUESTIONS -->
- What's been with you today, even if "nothing" is the honest answer.
- What does the numbness feel like, from inside it.
- Where in your body is it sitting.
- Is the numbness new, or has it been here for a while.
- When did you notice you were here.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: NUMBNESS_REGISTRY_QUESTIONS -->
- Is the numbness covering something specific, or is it general.
- If the numbness lifted for one second, what's the first thing that would be underneath.
- What do you imagine you'd feel if you were able to feel.
- Is this the kind of numbness that lifts on its own with time, or the kind that doesn't.
- Have you been here before. How long did it take to come back, then.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: NUMBNESS_FUNCTION_QUESTIONS -->
- What's the numbness protecting you from right now.
- What were you carrying right before this set in.
- What would have been too much to feel without breaking something.
- Is there a part of the numbness that's been useful this week.
- Is there a part that's been costing you.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: NUMBNESS_CONTACT_QUESTIONS -->
- Is there anyone in your life right now who knows you're here.
- Who's the easiest person to be around when you're numb.
- What's the kind of contact that doesn't require you to perform feeling.
- What's the kind of activity that lands when nothing else does — even small.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: NUMBNESS_CARE_QUESTIONS -->
- What does basic care look like from here this week.
- What's a thing you used to enjoy that you've stopped doing.
- What's a small thing you could give yourself permission to do today, without it having to mean anything.
- Is there a part of you that's been waiting for the numbness to lift before doing anything.
<!-- /SECTION -->
