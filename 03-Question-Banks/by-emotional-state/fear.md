# Fear — Question Bank

For use when the user is afraid — anticipated loss, anticipated
exposure, anticipated failure, anticipated harm. Bank applies
regardless of phase.

**Pacing rule.** One question per turn. Fear narrows attention; the
question must fit inside the narrowed window.

**Voice discipline.** No reassurance. Reassurance under fear reads
as dismissal — "you don't actually need to feel this." Hold the
fear; name it; refuse to either amplify or dilute.

<!-- SECTION: FEAR_NAMING_QUESTIONS -->
- What's the fear about, in plain words.
- What's the version of this that you have to be careful saying out loud.
- Where in the body is the fear sitting right now.
- What's the picture in your mind when the fear is loudest.
- If the fear had one sentence to say to you, what would it be.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: FEAR_SPECIFICITY_QUESTIONS -->
- What specifically would happen if the thing you're afraid of happened.
- What would you lose. What would change. Who would know.
- How likely is it, in your honest read.
- Who would be the first to feel the consequences besides you.
- What's the worst version of the consequences. What's the realistic version.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: FEAR_HISTORY_QUESTIONS -->
- Have you been afraid of this exact thing before.
- What happened the last time fear of this size showed up for you.
- Is the fear new, or is it an old fear in a new shape.
- What's the part of you that this fear is protecting.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: FEAR_RECOVERY_QUESTIONS -->
- If the thing happened, what would you do the day after.
- Who would you call.
- What resource would you reach for first.
- What's already true about you that would still be true if the worst happened.
- What's the version of you that has handled fear of this shape before.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: FEAR_PRESENT_QUESTIONS -->
- What's the thing you can actually do today about the part of this that's actionable.
- What's the thing that isn't actionable that you've been treating as if it were.
- What's a way you've been moving through the fear that's been working.
- What's the question you've been trying not to ask yourself.
- What do you need to hear from someone right now that you haven't asked for.
<!-- /SECTION -->
