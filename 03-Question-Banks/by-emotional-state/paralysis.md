# Paralysis — Question Bank

For use when the user is stuck — unable to move, can see what to do
and cannot do it, can't see what to do, all options feel equally
weighty or equally impossible. Bank applies regardless of phase.

**Pacing rule.** One question per turn. Paralysis is a held state;
rapid questioning reads as another thing the user is failing to
respond to.

**Voice discipline.** No "just pick one." No "any move is better
than no move." Paralysis is data; the questions help the user
read why they cannot move, not bypass the reason.

<!-- SECTION: PARALYSIS_NAMING_QUESTIONS -->
- What does the stuck feel like, in your body.
- Where in your week did the stuckness become impossible to ignore.
- What's the thing you've been telling yourself about being stuck.
- Is the paralysis silent or noisy.
- If you had to draw the shape of it, what shape would it be.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PARALYSIS_DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTIONS -->
- Is this paralysis of "I don't know what to do," or of "I know what to do and can't do it."
- If it's the second — what does the part of you that won't do it know that the rest of you doesn't.
- Is there a part of moving that feels like it would cost you something. What.
- Are you protecting someone or something by staying still.
- Is there a choice that the paralysis is keeping you from having to make.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PARALYSIS_FEAR_QUESTIONS -->
- Is there fear under this. Of what.
- What's the worst that could happen if you moved in any direction.
- What's the worst that could happen if you stayed still.
- Which worst feels worse.
- What's the thing about either move that would make you feel exposed.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PARALYSIS_PERMISSION_QUESTIONS -->
- Whose permission have you been waiting for.
- What would it take to stop waiting for it.
- Is there a version of moving that doesn't require having figured out the whole arc.
- What's the smallest visible move from here. Not the right move. The smallest visible one.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PARALYSIS_HISTORY_QUESTIONS -->
- Have you been here before. When.
- What got you moving the last time.
- What's different about now.
- Is the stuckness about this situation, or about something older that this situation is touching.
<!-- /SECTION -->
