# Elation — Question Bank

For use when the user is elated — high, charged, lifted, riding the
state of a win or a breakthrough or a relief. Bank applies
regardless of phase.

**Pacing rule.** One question per turn. Elation has its own
momentum; let the user ride. Multi-question turns risk reading as
"come down off this."

**Voice discipline — load-bearing.** Elation banks must not
deflate. The work is not to bring the user down to baseline; the
work is to register what's true in the elation that will still be
true tomorrow. The questions surface sustainability without
puncturing the state.

A useful frame: the elation contains real information about what
matters to the user, what worked, what's been carried. The
questions help the user hold the information past the chemical
window.

<!-- SECTION: ELATION_NAMING_QUESTIONS -->
- What's the elation about, in your own words.
- Where in your body is it sitting.
- What's the picture in your mind right now.
- What's the word the elation wants you to use.
- What does the day look like, on the inside, from here.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: ELATION_INFORMATION_QUESTIONS -->
- What just got confirmed for you that you weren't sure about.
- What worked here that you've been worried wouldn't work.
- What got reflected back to you that you needed to see.
- What's the part of this that's about you specifically, and what's the part that's about timing or luck.
- Who else made this possible. Have you said it to them.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: ELATION_REGISTER_QUESTIONS -->
- What's the thing you want to hold onto from this once the high passes.
- What part of this is durable. What part is the moment itself.
- If you could send a message to your next-week-self about what just happened, what would you say.
- What's the lesson in here that you'd want to remember even on a flat day.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: ELATION_SHARING_QUESTIONS -->
- Who do you want to tell first.
- Who do you want to tell quietly, just so it's witnessed.
- Is there anyone you're keeping this from, and why.
- What's the kind of celebration this calls for.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: ELATION_NEXT_QUESTIONS -->
- What does this make possible that wasn't possible yesterday.
- What does this not change.
- What's the work that gets easier from here. What's the work that doesn't.
- What's the question this win opens up that you didn't have before.
<!-- /SECTION -->
