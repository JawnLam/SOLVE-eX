# Shame — Question Bank

For use when the user is in shame — exposure, smallness,
self-as-defective, the impulse to hide. Bank applies regardless of
phase.

**Pacing rule.** One question per turn. Slow pacing. Shame is
already speaking too loudly; the AI must not add weight.

**Voice discipline — load-bearing.** Shame banks must not re-shame.
Questions cannot read as accusation, exposure, or evaluation. No
"why did you do that," no "what were you thinking." The questions
honor the user's report without confirming the shame's verdict
about who the user is.

The shame's verdict is "I am bad." The work is to disaggregate the
verdict — the *act*, the *circumstance*, the *judge*, the *self*.

<!-- SECTION: SHAME_NAMING_QUESTIONS -->
- What's the part of this you've been carrying alone.
- Where in your body is the shame sitting right now.
- What's the version of this story you've been telling yourself.
- What's the word the shame keeps using when it talks.
- If the shame had a voice, whose voice is it.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: SHAME_DISAGGREGATE_QUESTIONS -->
- What's the act, in plain language, without the verdict on you.
- What were the circumstances. What were you carrying that day. What had been hard already.
- Who's judging you, in your head. Is it actually them, or is it you using their voice.
- What's the difference between "I did a thing" and "I am the thing."
- If a friend told you this exact story about themselves, what would you say to them.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: SHAME_COMPANY_QUESTIONS -->
- Is there anyone who knows the full shape of this.
- What would change if one person knew.
- What's the thing you imagine they'd think if they knew, and is that an actual prediction or a fear.
- Who in your life has been honest about something like this with you, and is still loved.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: SHAME_HISTORY_QUESTIONS -->
- Have you felt this size of shame before, in your life.
- What did you learn the last time this voice was loud.
- Is the shame older than the situation you're in right now.
- What does the shame's voice sound like in your earliest memory of it.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: SHAME_REPAIR_QUESTIONS -->
- What would make-it-right look like, if make-it-right is even possible.
- What part of this is yours to repair, and what part is yours to carry.
- What would you need to forgive yourself for, even if no one else weighs in.
- Is there a small move you could make this week that would loosen the grip.
<!-- /SECTION -->
