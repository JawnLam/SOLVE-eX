# Grief — Question Bank

For use when the user is in grief — loss of a person, a role, a
relationship, a future, a version of themselves. Bank applies
regardless of phase.

**Pacing rule.** One question per turn. Maximum. Grief is held, not
worked through. If the user goes quiet, hold the silence. Do not
fill it.

**Voice discipline.** No fixing. No silver-lining reframes. No
"at least." No "what would they want for you." The questions
surface what the user is carrying; they do not redirect the user
out of carrying it.

<!-- SECTION: GRIEF_OPENING_QUESTIONS -->
- What's been with you today.
- What part of this is loudest right now.
- Where in your body is the grief sitting at the moment.
- What were you doing when it last came up for you today.
- What's the part of this that's hardest to say out loud.
- If you didn't have to be coherent — what would you say.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: GRIEF_PRESENCE_QUESTIONS -->
- Tell me one specific thing about what you've lost.
- What's something small you used to share with them.
- What did the ordinary days feel like.
- When you think about them, what's the moment that comes first.
- What's the thing you'd say to them if you could.
- What's the thing they'd say to you that you can still hear.
- What's missing from your week now that wasn't missing before.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: GRIEF_PERMISSION_QUESTIONS -->
- Is there a feeling about this you've been editing out of how you talk about it.
- Is there anyone you can be all the way honest with about this.
- Have you been allowed to be not-okay anywhere this week.
- What's the version of this that you usually keep to yourself.
- When you cry about this, where are you when it happens.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: GRIEF_DURATION_QUESTIONS -->
- How long has this been with you.
- Are there parts of the day where it's heavier or lighter.
- What does it feel like when it's heaviest.
- What does it feel like when it's lightest.
- Is there a part of yourself that you've also been missing since this happened.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: GRIEF_CARRY_QUESTIONS -->
- What's a way you've been carrying this that's been working.
- What's a way you've been carrying this that hasn't been.
- What do you need right now that you wouldn't ask anyone for.
<!-- /SECTION -->
