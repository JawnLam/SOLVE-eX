# Stuck Recovery — Question Bank

Meta-question bank for use when the AI detects the session has
stalled — multiple turns without progress, the same content getting
re-circulated, the user not yet able to commit or even articulate.
Bank applies at any phase.

**Pacing rule.** Sparingly. One stuck-recovery question in a
session is usually plenty. If the second one is needed within the
same session, the diagnostic itself may be wrong — back up rather
than asking again.

**Voice discipline — load-bearing.** Diagnostic, not motivational.
These questions surface *why* the session is stuck — they do not
push the user out of stuckness with motivational framing. "What's
one small step you could take" is motivational; "what would you be
willing to look at that you've been avoiding" is diagnostic. The
bank holds to diagnostic.

The distinction matters because motivational framings under
stuckness usually intensify the stuckness — the user adds "and I
can't even do the small step" to the existing pile. Diagnostic
framings give the user a way to read what the stuckness is doing.

The stuck-recovery bank is distinct from `paralysis.md` (the
emotional-state bank). Paralysis is the user's state; stuck-
recovery is the session's pattern. They overlap but are not the
same — a session can stall without the user being in paralysis
(over-diagnostic loop, missing variable, persona mismatch).

<!-- SECTION: STUCK_RECOVERY_DIAGNOSTIC_QUESTIONS -->
- What are we not talking about that should be on the table.
- What's the part of this you've been editing out of the conversation.
- What would I need to know that I haven't asked.
- Where in what we've covered did you feel something move, even slightly.
- Where in what we've covered did you feel something close down.
- What's the question I should have asked an hour ago.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: STUCK_RECOVERY_FRAME_QUESTIONS -->
- Is the question we're working on the right question.
- What's the question underneath the question we've been asking.
- If the actual problem is different from what we've been treating as the problem, what might it be.
- Have we been working on the symptom or the cause.
- Is there a frame for this that we haven't tried.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: STUCK_RECOVERY_PROCESS_QUESTIONS -->
- Is the pacing working for you.
- Is the format working for you — would you rather we sketch this differently.
- Have I been asking too much, or not enough.
- What kind of help would actually be useful right now, that I haven't offered.
- Is there a tool or a frame you've been wanting to use that I haven't suggested.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: STUCK_RECOVERY_HONESTY_QUESTIONS -->
- What have you been trying to make true about your answer that isn't true.
- What's the part of your situation you wish were different than it is.
- What would you say if you weren't trying to make this conversation go well.
- What would your closest friend, who knows the full story, say is being avoided here.
<!-- /SECTION -->
