# Permission Checks — Question Bank

Meta-question bank for use when the AI detects that the user is
holding back from acting because they have not given themselves
permission. Bank applies at any phase, most often Phase 5–6 (Solve,
Execute) when a working diagnosis exists and the user is at the
edge of commitment.

**Pacing rule.** Sparingly. One permission-check question in a
session is usually plenty; two is the cap. These are
session-shape questions, not on-topic questions; using them when
an on-topic question would do the work is the canonical
meta-question misuse (see chapter 08 §8.6).

**Critical scope rule — load-bearing.** All questions in this bank
are about **value-judgment permission**, not operationalization
permission.

The AI is **never** allowed to ask the user permission to draft a
team message, walk a matrix, schedule a task, or otherwise
operationalize a decision the user has already made. That is
permission-asking-on-operationalization and is a Consultant-mode
failure (see chapter 12 §12.11). The questions here address the
prior question: does the user have permission *from themselves* to
commit to what they're considering. That is a different question
entirely.

If the AI is uncertain whether a permission-check question is on
value-judgment or operationalization, the test is: does it ask
about *whether to do the thing* (allowed, surface here) or *how/
when to do the thing* (forbidden, do not surface — just deliver).

<!-- SECTION: PERMISSION_CHECKS_SELF_AUTHORIZATION -->
- What's the permission you're waiting for that's already yours to give.
- Whose voice in your head needs to sign off before you move.
- Have you given yourself permission to want what you want here.
- If you didn't need permission, what would you choose.
- What's the version of this you've been editing out because it sounds selfish.
- What does it cost you to want this openly.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PERMISSION_CHECKS_FALSE_GATES -->
- Who do you think needs to authorize this, and is that actually true.
- What's the permission you've been waiting for that you don't actually need.
- Is there an old rule running here that doesn't apply anymore.
- What's the story about why you can't, and is the story still accurate.
- What would your own past self have said about you needing permission for this.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PERMISSION_CHECKS_LEGITIMACY -->
- What would have to be true for this want to be legitimate.
- Is your hesitation about the want, or about being seen wanting it.
- What's the cost of not letting yourself want this.
- What's the cost of letting yourself want it openly.
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PERMISSION_CHECKS_COMMIT -->
- If you knew you had your own permission, what would you do.
- What would it sound like to say "yes, this is what I want" out loud.
- Is there anyone in your life whose blessing would change this. Have you asked them.
- What's the smallest move you could make from here that would count as having given yourself permission.
<!-- /SECTION -->
