# Phase 3.4 — Find Future Facts and Data

For Step 3.4: trends, projections, scenarios — what is likely or possible going forward.

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_3_4_OPENING_QUESTIONS -->
- What's likely to be true in 6 months that isn't true now?
- What's the trend in this situation, if any?
- What's a near-term event that will shift the picture?
- What's a longer-term shift you're betting on, or against?
- What's the worst-case scenario? Best-case? Most likely?
- What does the future look like if nothing changes?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_3_4_CLARIFYING_QUESTIONS -->
- What demographic, market, technological, or regulatory shifts are relevant?
- What's a forecast someone you trust has made about this domain?
- What's a scenario you'd find surprising, and how likely is it?
- What's the speed of the change — fast (months), medium (1–3 years), slow (5+ years)?
- What's a counterfactual — what if this situation hadn't surfaced now, when would it have?
- What's the path of least change versus most change?
- What inputs from now would change the future trajectory?
- What's the part of the future that's mostly determined already, versus genuinely open?
- What signals would tell you a scenario is unfolding?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_3_4_STRESS_TEST_QUESTIONS -->
- How confident are you in the trends you stated, on a scale of 1–10?
- What's a confidently-held forecast you've gotten wrong before, and is this similar?
- Are you projecting from a small sample of recent events?
- Is the future you're imagining the most likely one, or the most vivid one?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_3_4_REFRAMING_QUESTIONS -->
- What's the future from the perspective of someone outside this situation?
- What's the future if the dominant assumption breaks?
- What's the future if the system stays stable but you change?
- What's the future if you stay the same but the system changes?
- Imagine running this forward as three distinct scenarios — what's true in each?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_3_4_CLOSING_QUESTIONS -->
- Do we have enough of a sense of the future to factor it in?
- What forecast would you want to revisit if it turned out to be wrong?
- Ready to move to Phase 4 and generate paths?
<!-- /SECTION -->
