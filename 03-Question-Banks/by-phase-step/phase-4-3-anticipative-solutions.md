# Phase 4.3 — Anticipative Solution Crafting

For Step 4.3: crafting solutions that account for second-order effects and likely futures.

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_4_3_OPENING_QUESTIONS -->
- For each option, what happens after it works?
- Which option is robust to the most futures?
- Which option only works if certain things go right?
- What's the option that anticipates the response it will provoke?
- What's the option designed for the worst case?
- What's the option designed for the most-likely case?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_4_3_CLARIFYING_QUESTIONS -->
- For each option, what's a likely second-order effect?
- What's a third-order effect that might be more important than the first-order one?
- Who else reacts to each option, and how?
- What's an option that closes future options, and what's one that preserves them?
- What's an option that is reversible if it doesn't work?
- What's an option that costs little to try and tells you a lot?
- What's the worst likely outcome of each option?
- What's the best likely outcome of each option?
- Which option, looking back from 5 years out, would you wish you'd taken?
- Which option, looking back, would you regret?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_4_3_STRESS_TEST_QUESTIONS -->
- Imagine each option failed catastrophically. What was the failure mode?
- What option are you mentally over-protecting because you've already committed to it?
- What's an option you'd be embarrassed to choose, and is that the reason it's getting screened out?
- For each option, what assumption does it rest on that, if wrong, breaks it?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_4_3_REFRAMING_QUESTIONS -->
- What option works in the most futures, even if it's not optimal in any single one?
- What option assumes the future you most want, and what does it assume?
- What option assumes the future you most fear?
- What option is most flexible — i.e., lets you respond to new information as it arrives?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_4_3_CLOSING_QUESTIONS -->
- Have we crafted the options to a level that lets us evaluate them?
- Are the options different enough from each other to be a real choice?
- Ready to refine the strongest ones?
<!-- /SECTION -->
