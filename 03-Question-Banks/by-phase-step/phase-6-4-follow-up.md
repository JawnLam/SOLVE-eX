# Phase 6.4 — Conduct Follow-up and Feedback

For Step 6.4: post-action review — did it work, what changed, what was learned.

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_6_4_OPENING_QUESTIONS -->
- Looking back, did the plan work?
- What was the outcome — better, worse, or different than expected?
- What was learned along the way?
- What was the surprise — pleasant or otherwise?
- What part of the original problem is now resolved?
- What part of the original problem is still active?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_6_4_CLARIFYING_QUESTIONS -->
- What worked that you'd repeat next time?
- What didn't work that you'd skip next time?
- What was harder than expected?
- What was easier than expected?
- What did you learn about yourself in the process?
- What did you learn about the situation in the process?
- Who turned out to be a key collaborator, ally, or obstacle?
- What's the lesson that transfers to other decisions?
- What's the lesson that's specific to this one and doesn't generalize?
- What's still unresolved, and what does it need?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_6_4_STRESS_TEST_QUESTIONS -->
- Are you giving yourself accurate credit or accurate blame?
- Are you romanticizing the outcome because you wanted it to work?
- Are you under-counting partial wins?
- Is your assessment matching what other involved people see, or diverging?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_6_4_REFRAMING_QUESTIONS -->
- How would a coach assess the outcome?
- How would a critic assess it?
- If you were writing a case study on this for someone else's benefit, what would the key takeaway be?
- What would you do differently if you ran it again?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_6_4_CLOSING_QUESTIONS -->
- Is the case file ready to mark resolved, or is there still work to do?
- Anything you want to capture in writing for future-you to know about this?
- Is there a follow-on question you want to open as a new frame?
<!-- /SECTION -->
