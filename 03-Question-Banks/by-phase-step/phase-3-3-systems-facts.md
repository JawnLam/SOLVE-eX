# Phase 3.3 — Find Systems Facts and Data

For Step 3.3: how the surrounding system works — structures, incentives, feedback loops.

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_3_3_OPENING_QUESTIONS -->
- What's the system the situation sits inside?
- Who or what creates the pressure that put you here?
- What incentives are operating in this situation, and on whom?
- What's the feedback loop — what's reinforcing the current state?
- What rules or structures shape how this situation plays out?
- Who has authority over which parts of this?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_3_3_CLARIFYING_QUESTIONS -->
- What stakeholders are involved, and what does each one want?
- What's the formal structure (org chart, contract, law) versus the informal structure (who actually gets things done)?
- What are the financial flows in or out?
- What's the time pressure (deadlines, calendar, lifecycle)?
- What policies or norms apply that you didn't choose but must work within?
- What's the broader environment (market, industry, community) doing?
- What's an analogous system from elsewhere that helps you see this one more clearly?
- What's a part of the system that's invisible most of the time?
- What's the system's normal mode of operation, and where is the current situation an exception?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_3_3_STRESS_TEST_QUESTIONS -->
- Is the system you described how it really is, or how it presents itself?
- Are you assuming a stakeholder's behavior or have you confirmed it?
- What's a piece of the system that operates differently than your model says?
- Are there second-order effects you're not yet modeling?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_3_3_REFRAMING_QUESTIONS -->
- How would a systems thinker describe this — with stocks, flows, delays, feedback?
- How would an anthropologist describe this — with norms, rituals, power?
- How would an economist describe this — with incentives, costs, equilibria?
- Where in the system is the leverage point — the place where small action produces big effect?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_3_3_CLOSING_QUESTIONS -->
- Do we have a useful enough model of the system?
- Is there a system fact still missing that would change how you think about paths?
- Ready to look at future trends and projections next?
<!-- /SECTION -->
