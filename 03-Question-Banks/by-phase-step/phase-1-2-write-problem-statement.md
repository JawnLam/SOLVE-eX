# Phase 1.2 — Write the Problem Statement

For Step 1.2: compressing the situation into a one-sentence problem statement.

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_1_2_OPENING_QUESTIONS -->
- If you had to compress this into one sentence, what would it be?
- What's the headline?
- Fill in: "The problem is ___."
- What's the part that, if it were different, you wouldn't be here?
- What's the smallest thing that, when named, captures the most of the situation?
- What would you tell someone if you had only 30 seconds to describe it?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_1_2_CLARIFYING_QUESTIONS -->
- Is what you wrote a problem, a symptom, or a complaint?
- Whose problem is this — yours, someone else's, or shared?
- What's the verb at the heart of the problem (e.g., "I am stuck," "I cannot decide," "I am losing")?
- What's the noun at the heart (e.g., a relationship, a job, a habit, a decision)?
- Is the problem ongoing or one-shot?
- Is the problem getting better, getting worse, or stable?
- What would your statement sound like if you stripped away the emotion?
- What would it sound like if you kept the emotion?
- Does the statement describe a state, an event, or a pattern?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_1_2_STRESS_TEST_QUESTIONS -->
- Is there a more accurate problem statement underneath the one you just wrote?
- If the problem you stated were solved tomorrow, would things actually be different?
- Could two reasonable people read your statement and disagree about what the problem is?
- What's hidden in the statement that you'd want to bring forward if pressed?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_1_2_REFRAMING_QUESTIONS -->
- What's the problem statement that someone older than you would write about the same situation?
- What's the version that's most concrete?
- What's the version that's most about your inner experience?
- What's the version that's most about the outer circumstances?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_1_2_CLOSING_QUESTIONS -->
- Does the statement you have feel like the problem, or only part of it?
- Are you ready to use this as the problem we work on?
<!-- /SECTION -->
