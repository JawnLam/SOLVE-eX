# Phase 4.4 — Idea Refinement

For Step 4.4: sharpening, combining, hybridizing options into the shortlist for evaluation.

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_4_4_OPENING_QUESTIONS -->
- Which two or three options are the strongest?
- What's the strongest version of each?
- Can any options be combined into a hybrid?
- What's the next-detail-level of each option?
- What part of each option is rock-solid, and what part is hand-wavy?
- What would make each option clearly viable?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_4_4_CLARIFYING_QUESTIONS -->
- For each option, what's the concrete next step if you took it?
- For each, what's the timeline?
- For each, what's the cost?
- For each, who else needs to agree?
- For each, what skill or resource do you not have yet?
- Which option can be tried in a small form to learn before fully committing?
- Which option has a precedent (someone has done something similar)?
- Which option is uniquely yours — no template fits exactly?
- Which option could be sequenced (do A first, then B based on what you learn)?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_4_4_STRESS_TEST_QUESTIONS -->
- For your strongest option: what's the weakest link?
- Have you been romanticizing one option over the others?
- Have you been dismissing one option for the wrong reason?
- Is the difference between your top options real, or are they basically the same option with different labels?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_4_4_REFRAMING_QUESTIONS -->
- What's a refined option that combines the best of two?
- What's an option presented in its fully-realized, polished form versus its rough form?
- What's the option you'd take after a sabbatical, with a fresh head?
- What's the option that a wiser, calmer version of you would take?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_4_4_CLOSING_QUESTIONS -->
- Do we have a refined shortlist of 2–4 options?
- Are these the options you actually want to evaluate against criteria?
- Ready to move to Phase 5 evaluation?
<!-- /SECTION -->
