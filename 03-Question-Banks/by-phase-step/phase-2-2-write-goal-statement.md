# Phase 2.2 — Write the Goal Statement

For Step 2.2: a coherent statement of the desired outcome.

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_2_2_OPENING_QUESTIONS -->
- What's the outcome you're working toward?
- If this worked out, what would you have, be, or feel?
- Fill in: "The goal is ___."
- What's "good enough" look like here?
- What's a great outcome — beyond just acceptable?
- Picture yourself in the future where this is done. What's true about that picture?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_2_2_CLARIFYING_QUESTIONS -->
- Is the goal a state, an event, a capability, or a relationship?
- What does "achieved" look like — by whose judgment?
- When you say "X," what do you mean specifically?
- What's the level of ambition — minimal, moderate, ambitious, breakthrough?
- What's the timeline — weeks, months, years?
- Who's the audience for the goal — just you, or are others stakeholders?
- What's the unit of progress — is there a measurable indicator?
- Is the goal singular or plural?
- What does the next layer of detail look like underneath the headline goal?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_2_2_STRESS_TEST_QUESTIONS -->
- Is this goal yours, or is it one you think you should have?
- Six months from now, would you still want this in this exact form?
- If you achieved this and nothing else changed, would it actually feel like success?
- Is there a hidden assumption in the goal that, if false, would change it?
- What's the goal you'd state if you were less worried about looking foolish?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_2_2_REFRAMING_QUESTIONS -->
- What's the goal expressed as moving toward, instead of away from?
- What's the goal expressed at a higher level of generality?
- What's the goal expressed at a more concrete level?
- What's the goal in different words entirely?
- What's the goal that the goal you stated is pointing toward?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_2_2_CLOSING_QUESTIONS -->
- Does this feel like yours when you read it back?
- Are you willing to use this as the destination for the rest of the work?
- Anything to add or sharpen before locking it?
<!-- /SECTION -->
