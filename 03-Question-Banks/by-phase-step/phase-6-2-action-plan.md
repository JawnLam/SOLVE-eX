# Phase 6.2 — Create Action Plan

For Step 6.2: concrete time-bound plan.

**See also:**
- `phase-6-1-design-test.md` — small experiments that de-risk the plan before commitment.
- `phase-6-3-implementation.md` — what happens once execution begins.
- `phase-6-4-follow-up.md` — the review cadence that catches plan-vs-reality drift.
- `meta-questions/probing-questions.md` — when an action-plan answer is too generic to act on.
- `by-clarification-need/clarification-on-action.md` — when the plan-question itself needs unpacking.

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_6_2_OPENING_QUESTIONS -->
- What's step one, in concrete terms?
- When does step one happen?
- What's the date by which step one must be done?
- What are the next 3–5 steps?
- What's the rhythm — daily, weekly, monthly?
- What's the milestone you'll watch for?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_6_2_CLARIFYING_QUESTIONS -->
- Who else has a role in the plan?
- What does each step require — resources, tools, information?
- What's the cost (money, time, energy) of each step?
- What's the dependency order — what must happen before what?
- What's the trigger that moves you from step N to step N+1?
- What's the contingency if step N takes longer than planned?
- What's the contingency if step N fails entirely?
- How will you protect time for this from competing priorities?
- What's the routine or system that will keep you on the plan when motivation dips?
- What's the part of the plan that depends on something outside your control?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_6_2_STRESS_TEST_QUESTIONS -->
- Is the plan realistic given everything else on your plate?
- Have you been here before with a plan like this — and what happened?
- Where in the plan is the slip-zone (the place where past plans have died)?
- Is the plan ambitious enough, or are you under-shooting?
- Who else pressure-tests this interview/evaluation output before commitment?
<!-- /SECTION -->

## Annotated question — "Who else pressure-tests this?"

**Trigger.** Founder or single-decider case where the upcoming
commitment is high-stakes and the evaluation channel is narrow —
the decider personally interviewed the candidate, personally read
the deal terms, personally evaluated the partnership. Highest
trigger when the case combines (a) charismatic counterparty,
(b) slow-ramp / long-tail commitment, (c) decider has no
structural pressure-test loop in place.

**Why this question.** Founders' interview-room blind spots are
well-documented: charisma overweighting, missed red flags,
mistaking confidence for competence, sympathetic-stranger bias.
Single-decider judgments on charismatic candidates correlate
poorly with subsequent performance outcomes. Asking "who else
sees this before commitment" surfaces whether the decider has a
structural pressure-test loop OR is single-pointed.

**Common answers and what they mean.**

| Answer pattern | What it signals | Move |
|----------------|----------------|------|
| *"No one — I'll decide."* | High single-decider risk. The decider is the only filter; no structural correction for in-room bias. | Suggest at least one structural pressure-test before commitment: reference check with someone who would tell the truth; second-interviewer with explicitly skeptical mandate; written-evaluation step that forces the decider to articulate criteria before warm impressions consolidate. |
| *"Co-founder / partner / advisor."* | Pressure-test exists structurally, but the pressure-tester's incentive matters. | Ask: are they incentivized to spot the failure, or to confirm the decision? Pressure-tests work only when the pressure-tester is incentivized to find the problem, not to support the decider's choice. |
| *"Spouse / mentor / friend."* | Valuable for character read; rarely substitutes for domain-expert pressure-test. | Pair with a domain-expert pressure-test specifically on the technical / commercial / operational dimensions of the commitment. |

**Companion tool.** When the answer surfaces high single-decider
risk on a commitment with slow-ramp / charisma / sunk-cost
exposure, pair this question with **Pre-Commitment Gate**
(`01-Tools/Tool Entries/Pre-Commitment Gate.md`): the question
identifies the structural gap; the Pre-Commitment Gate ritual is
the operational response when the gap cannot be closed before
commitment.

**Source.** Sprint 11 Card 07 — emerged organically during
Sprint 10 panel testing on the Tessa Hollis hire-vs-stay case.
The founder's interview-room blind spot was named but not
captured in the formal library until Sprint 11.

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_6_2_REFRAMING_QUESTIONS -->
- What's the plan if you couldn't do anything new — only redirect what you're already doing?
- What's the plan if you cut the timeline in half?
- What's the plan if you doubled the timeline?
- What's the plan that another person who's done this would write?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_6_2_ACCOUNTABILITY_PROBES -->
- Who knows the plan besides you?
- Who's the person you'd be embarrassed to tell if the plan slipped?
- What's the public-commitment level of the plan — private, shared with one person, shared with many?
- What's the cadence for telling someone else about progress?
- If you missed step N by a week, who would notice first?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_6_2_FRICTION_PROBES -->
- Where in the plan does the first hour of work happen?
- What's the first action that requires zero permission from anyone else?
- What's the friction at the start of the plan — what makes step 1 hard to begin?
- What's the routine you'd disrupt to make room for step 1?
- What's already on your calendar this week that supports or competes with the plan?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_6_2_FAILURE_RECOVERY_PROBES -->
- If the plan fails at step N, what does "graceful failure" look like vs. "catastrophic failure"?
- What's the smallest signal that the plan is off course?
- What's the trigger that says "stop executing, re-plan"?
- Who do you call when the plan is breaking and you need a second pair of eyes?
- What's the part of the plan that you're hoping won't happen the way you've planned it?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_6_2_CLOSING_QUESTIONS -->
- Does the plan feel doable when you sit with it for a moment?
- When and how will you check on whether you're on track?
- What's the smallest version of step 1 that you'd commit to right now?
- Anything to add or sharpen before treating this as the plan?
<!-- /SECTION -->
