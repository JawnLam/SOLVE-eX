# Phase 2.3 — Establish Requirements

For Step 2.3: the non-negotiable criteria for any acceptable outcome.

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_2_3_OPENING_QUESTIONS -->
- What does any acceptable outcome have to include?
- What's the absolute floor?
- Fill in: "I won't accept anything that ___."
- What's the minimum bar?
- What does "no, that doesn't count" look like?
- What's the dealbreaker?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_2_3_CLARIFYING_QUESTIONS -->
- Which requirements are non-negotiable, and which are strong preferences?
- Where did each requirement come from — values, constraints, past experience?
- Are any of the requirements in tension with each other?
- What's the requirement you'd be embarrassed to admit to in front of certain people?
- What's a requirement you might be over-weighting?
- What requirement is missing that should be there?
- Are there requirements about how you reach the outcome, not just the outcome itself?
- What's required by you alone, and what's required by your context (family, work, etc.)?
- What's the requirement you wish you didn't have?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_2_3_STRESS_TEST_QUESTIONS -->
- If you had to drop one requirement, which would it be, and what does that say?
- Are these requirements yours, or inherited?
- What's the cost of holding each requirement firm?
- If a clearly-good outcome failed one requirement, would you still walk away from it?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_2_3_REFRAMING_QUESTIONS -->
- What's a requirement that, when stated, surprises you?
- What would the requirements look like if framed positively (must include) vs. negatively (must avoid)?
- What's the deepest requirement underneath the surface requirements?
- What requirements does the person you most respect have for their own life?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_2_3_CLOSING_QUESTIONS -->
- Do these requirements feel complete?
- Are any of them likely to drop or shift as you learn more?
- Ready to use these as the test bed for any option we generate?
<!-- /SECTION -->
