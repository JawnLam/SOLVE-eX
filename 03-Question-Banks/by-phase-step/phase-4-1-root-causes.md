# Phase 4.1 — Analyze Root Causes

For Step 4.1: the gap-explaining causes — why is this happening?

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_4_1_OPENING_QUESTIONS -->
- Why is this happening?
- What's underneath the symptom you've been describing?
- If this were a tree, what's the root and what's the leaves?
- What's the cause of the cause?
- What's the simplest possible explanation for this gap?
- What's the most-complex-but-true explanation?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_4_1_CLARIFYING_QUESTIONS -->
- Run a Five Whys on this. Why does X happen? Why does that happen? Why does that? Why does that? Why does that?
- Are there multiple independent causes, or one root cause?
- Is the cause structural (built into the system), behavioral (someone's habits), or situational (a one-off)?
- Who or what would have to change for the root cause to be removed?
- Is the cause inside your control, partly in your control, or outside it?
- What's a part of the cause you've been minimizing?
- What's a part you've been over-weighting?
- What evidence backs the causal story you're telling?
- What would the cause look like if you described it in the third person?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_4_1_STRESS_TEST_QUESTIONS -->
- If your causal story is right, what would you predict happens next?
- What's a fact that, if true, would invalidate your causal model?
- Are you confusing correlation with causation anywhere?
- What's a cause you've ruled out — and why?
- If a friend told you a different cause, what would it be?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_4_1_REFRAMING_QUESTIONS -->
- What's the cause if we look at this as a systems problem?
- What's the cause if we look at this as an individual decision?
- What's the cause if we look at this as a values mismatch?
- What's the cause if we look at this as a timing issue?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_4_1_CLOSING_QUESTIONS -->
- Do we have a useful working model of the cause?
- Where is it solid, where is it tentative?
- Ready to start generating possible solutions?
<!-- /SECTION -->
