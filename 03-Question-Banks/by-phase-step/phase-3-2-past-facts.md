# Phase 3.2 — Find Past Facts and Data

For Step 3.2: historical context — what was true that explains how we got here.

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_3_2_OPENING_QUESTIONS -->
- How did we get here?
- What's the history of this situation, in broad strokes?
- What were the key moments that shaped where we are?
- What started this?
- What pattern, if any, has repeated over time?
- What changed that put us at this point?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_3_2_CLARIFYING_QUESTIONS -->
- What was the situation a year ago? Five years ago?
- What was tried before that didn't work?
- What was tried before that did work, partially or fully?
- Who has been involved across time, and what's been their pattern?
- What were the assumptions or commitments made earlier that still shape today?
- What's a decision made in the past that you'd revisit if you could?
- What's a decision made in the past that you wouldn't revisit?
- What evidence exists in writing, records, or memory of how prior choices played out?
- Are there cycles you've been through before in similar form?
- What lessons from past attempts apply here, and which don't transfer?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_3_2_STRESS_TEST_QUESTIONS -->
- Is your memory of the history the same as someone else's who was there?
- Is there a piece of history you've reconstructed rather than remembered?
- What's a part of the past you've been telling a clean story about that wasn't actually clean?
- Are you giving past events more weight than they deserve, or less?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_3_2_REFRAMING_QUESTIONS -->
- How would you tell this history if you removed yourself from it as a protagonist?
- How would you tell it if you were writing a case study on what works in situations like this?
- What's the version of the history that emphasizes external causes versus internal causes?
- What would the history look like if you ran it forward starting from different decision points?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_3_2_CLOSING_QUESTIONS -->
- Do we have enough of the history to understand the present?
- What's still uncertain about the past that might matter?
- Ready to look at systems and future facts next?
<!-- /SECTION -->
