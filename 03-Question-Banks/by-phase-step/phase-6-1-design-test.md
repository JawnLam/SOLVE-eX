# Phase 6.1 — Design and Test Solution

For Step 6.1: pilot, prototype, or low-cost experiment.

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_6_1_OPENING_QUESTIONS -->
- What's the smallest version of the chosen path you can try?
- What's the experiment that would teach you the most for the least cost?
- What's the prototype that captures the essence of the path?
- What's a pilot — a real but bounded trial?
- What's the test that, if it fails, you can absorb the loss?
- What's the test that, if it succeeds, tells you to scale up?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_6_1_CLARIFYING_QUESTIONS -->
- What's the budget (time, money, attention) for this test?
- What's the duration?
- What's the success criterion for the test specifically (different from the long-term success criterion)?
- What's the failure criterion?
- Who needs to be involved in the test?
- What's the data you'll gather from the test?
- What's the decision you'll make based on the test's outcome?
- What's the timeline from test to scale-up if it works?
- What's the next experiment if it fails?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_6_1_STRESS_TEST_QUESTIONS -->
- Is this test actually informative, or is it designed to confirm a foregone conclusion?
- What's a failure mode you've designed around but should design for?
- Is the test small enough that you'll actually run it, given everything else on your plate?
- Is the test too small to be informative?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_6_1_REFRAMING_QUESTIONS -->
- What's the test if you couldn't change anything in your life — just observe?
- What's the test if you could only do one small thing differently?
- What's the test if you committed to a 30-day trial?
- What's the test if you treated this as iterative — many small experiments in series?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_6_1_CLOSING_QUESTIONS -->
- Is the test designed in a way you can actually run?
- When does it start?
- When and how will you review the results?
<!-- /SECTION -->
