# Phase 4.2 — Expansive Idea Formation

For Step 4.2: surfacing a broad inventory of possible paths.

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_4_2_OPENING_QUESTIONS -->
- What could you do?
- What are all the options on the table?
- What's a wild option, just for variety?
- What's the obvious option you'd start with?
- What's an option you haven't been letting yourself consider?
- If you had unlimited resources, what would you do?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_4_2_CLARIFYING_QUESTIONS -->
- What's the option that other people in your life have been suggesting?
- What's an option from a totally different domain that might apply?
- What's the option that requires the least change?
- What's the option that requires the most change?
- What's an option that combines two existing ideas?
- What's an option you've ruled out — and is it worth reconsidering?
- What's an option that makes the situation worse before better?
- What's an option that defers the decision rather than making it?
- What's an option you'd take if no one was watching?
- What's the option you'd recommend to a friend in your shoes?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_4_2_STRESS_TEST_QUESTIONS -->
- Are you only generating options that fit the frame, or are some breaking the frame?
- What's an option you can't think of because it doesn't occur to you in your current mood?
- Are you anchoring on one option as obvious — and skipping the divergent work?
- Have you generated enough options to be sure the right one is in the set?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_4_2_REFRAMING_QUESTIONS -->
- What's the option that solves the cause, not just the symptom?
- What's the option that does the opposite of what you'd naturally do?
- What's the option that involves doing less, not more?
- What's the option that involves someone other than you doing something?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_4_2_CLOSING_QUESTIONS -->
- Do we have a wide enough set of options to start refining?
- Is there an obvious option we're missing?
- Ready to start refining and combining?
<!-- /SECTION -->
