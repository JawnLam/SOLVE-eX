# Phase 3.1 — Find Current Facts and Data

For Step 3.1: snapshot of the present state — what is actually true now.

**See also:**
- `meta-questions/probing-questions.md` — when an answer is thin and needs follow-up depth.
- `by-clarification-need/clarification-on-path.md` — when the answer shows confusion about *what's currently true* vs. *what should be done*.
- `phase-3-2-past-facts.md` — natural next-step after current facts land; the past facts that explain the present.
- `phase-1-1-describe-situation.md` — back-reference when the current-facts question surfaces gaps in the original situation description.
- `phase-4-1-root-causes.md` — forward-reference when current facts surface causal questions the analysis needs to chase.

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_3_1_OPENING_QUESTIONS -->
- What do you know for sure about the current situation?
- What are the facts on the ground right now?
- What's the data — numbers, measurements, observable things?
- Who are the parties involved, and what do you know about each?
- What resources are available right now?
- What are the current constraints, in concrete terms?
- Walk me through the current state in three or four bullet points — what's true today?
- If someone asked you to brief them on the current situation in 60 seconds, what would you say?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_3_1_CLARIFYING_QUESTIONS -->
- What do you know that's documented, versus what do you know that's inferred?
- What's a fact you've been treating as obvious but haven't actually verified?
- What numbers matter here, and what are they currently?
- What's the timeline — what milestones have already passed, and which haven't?
- Who has direct evidence about the current state, and have you spoken to them?
- What's the current status of any commitments, contracts, or arrangements?
- What's the current emotional/relational state with the key people involved?
- What's the current rate of change — is the situation stable, drifting, or accelerating?
- What's missing from your current map?
- What's the most recent data point you have on each of the key variables?
- Which of the current facts are widely shared (others would name them) vs. which are only visible from your vantage?
- What's the current operating tempo — daily decisions, weekly, monthly?
- What's the current resource burn rate (time, money, attention) on this?
- Which current facts have a half-life — they'll be different in a week, a month, a quarter?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_3_1_STRESS_TEST_QUESTIONS -->
- Which of the facts you stated would you bet money on, and which are more like impressions?
- What's a fact you've been assuming that, if untrue, changes everything?
- Where might your information be stale?
- Is there a fact you've been avoiding looking at directly?
- If we surveyed the other parties involved, where would their version of "the current facts" diverge from yours?
- What's a fact you can verify in the next 48 hours that would meaningfully change your read?
- What's a fact you currently treat as evidence but is actually a hypothesis you haven't tested?
- If a forensic auditor walked into the situation today, which of your facts would they double-check first?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_3_1_REFRAMING_QUESTIONS -->
- What would the situation look like from a stakeholder you haven't talked to?
- What would an auditor (external, dispassionate) say is true here?
- What's the same situation described in numbers versus described in words?
- What facts would matter for someone deciding the opposite of what you're considering?
- If you removed the labels and judgments, what raw observations remain?
- What's the current situation if you described only what an external camera would see?
- What's a fact that's true today that you wish were different — and what's the gap between is and want?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_3_1_CLOSING_QUESTIONS -->
- Do we have enough of the current facts to move forward?
- Is there a fact-gathering action that should happen before we go further?
- Where are the knowns solid, and where are the knowns shaky?
- What's the one current fact you wish you knew but don't?
- Of all the current facts, which is the load-bearing one — the one that, if wrong, changes the analysis?
- What's the time-cost of verifying the shakiest current fact, and is it worth it?
<!-- /SECTION -->
