# Phase 6.3 — Implement Action Plan

For Step 6.3: actually executing the plan.

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_6_3_OPENING_QUESTIONS -->
- What did you actually do this week?
- What got done?
- What didn't get done?
- What surprised you in the doing?
- What's the next concrete action this week?
- What's the cue or routine that's been working?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_6_3_CLARIFYING_QUESTIONS -->
- What got in the way of the steps that didn't happen?
- What was different from what you expected when you wrote the plan?
- Who else is doing their part, and where are they?
- What part of the plan is unfolding faster than expected?
- What part is slower?
- What's the cost (energy, time, money) so far versus what you planned?
- What's the early signal — is the plan working?
- What's an early warning sign that the plan isn't working?
- What's a small adjustment you'd make to keep things moving?
- What's something you need to stop doing to make room for this?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_6_3_STRESS_TEST_QUESTIONS -->
- Are you sticking to the plan, or drifting?
- Is the drift a problem, or is it adaptive?
- Is what you're calling "done" actually done, or just performed?
- Are you doing the easy parts of the plan and avoiding the hard parts?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_6_3_REFRAMING_QUESTIONS -->
- If you were coaching someone else through this plan, what would you tell them this week?
- What's the version of the next step that's small enough you'd take it today?
- What would the plan look like if you treated this week as a fresh start?
- What if you only had one hour for this — what's the action that earns the hour?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_6_3_CLOSING_QUESTIONS -->
- Is the plan still the right plan, or does it need revision?
- What's the next concrete step before our next conversation?
- Anything you want me to remember for next time we talk about this?
<!-- /SECTION -->
