# Phase 1.1 — Describe the Situation

For Step 1.1: building a coherent narrative of the current state.

**See also:**
- `meta-questions/probing-questions.md` — when an answer is thin and needs follow-up depth.
- `by-clarification-need/clarification-on-origin.md` — when the answer shows confusion about *where* the user is starting from.
- `phase-1-3-frame-situation.md` — natural next-step after the description lands; the frame the user is using to describe.

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_1_1_OPENING_QUESTIONS -->
- What's happening for you right now around this?
- Tell me what's going on in your own words.
- Walk me through what brought you here today.
- When you think about the situation, what's the first image or word that comes up?
- If you were describing this to a friend over coffee, where would you start?
- What's been on your mind that you haven't had a chance to say yet?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_1_1_CLARIFYING_QUESTIONS -->
- When did this begin to be something you had to actively think about?
- Who else is part of the picture?
- What's the part of the situation that's hardest to describe?
- What's happening that someone observing from the outside would notice?
- What's happening that an outside observer would miss?
- What constraints are you operating under right now?
- What's the part of your routine that this is most disrupting?
- What's something about the current state that you find yourself returning to mentally?
- What's the most recent event that captures what this is like?
- What roles are you playing in the situation (e.g., decider, witness, affected party)?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_1_1_STRESS_TEST_QUESTIONS -->
- If we asked you this same question tomorrow, would the description be the same?
- Where in what you said are you reporting facts, and where are you reporting interpretations?
- Is there a detail you left out because it complicates the story?
- If a friend pushed back and said "I think you're missing something," what might they mean?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_1_1_REFRAMING_QUESTIONS -->
- What's a one-sentence headline for what's happening?
- If we zoomed out a year, how would you describe this?
- What's the same situation described in different language — more concrete, or more emotional, or more strategic?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_1_1_CONSTRAINT_PROBES -->
- What's the constraint you keep bumping into?
- What's the constraint you've been treating as fixed but might not be?
- What's the resource (time, money, attention, relationships) that's tightest?
- What's the constraint that's invisible to most people but obvious to you?
- If one constraint disappeared overnight, which would it be — and what would change?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_1_1_EMOTIONAL_FRAME_PROBES -->
- What's the feeling underneath the description you just gave?
- What's the feeling you're trying not to put words to yet?
- What's the part that's hardest to talk about?
- What's the feeling that's been there longer than the situation has?
- If the emotional volume were turned down, would the description change?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_1_1_TIME_TEXTURE_PROBES -->
- Is this the latest version of an older story, or genuinely new?
- What changed recently that made this feel urgent or active?
- What's the slow change underneath the recent change?
- What's the version of this from a year ago?
- What's the version of this from a year from now, if nothing changed?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_1_1_CLOSING_QUESTIONS -->
- Does the description we've landed on feel accurate?
- Anything missing that we should add before moving on?
- What did you say out loud that you hadn't said out loud before?
- Ready to try compressing this into a problem statement?
<!-- /SECTION -->
