# Phase 5.3 — Validate Decision Impact

For Step 5.3: gut-check, second-order check, reversibility check.

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_5_3_OPENING_QUESTIONS -->
- Does the option that won the evaluation match your gut?
- If it doesn't, what's the gut saying?
- What's a way the evaluation could be wrong?
- What's a way your gut could be wrong?
- If you took this decision today, how would you feel tonight?
- If you took it, how would you feel one year from now?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_5_3_CLARIFYING_QUESTIONS -->
- What does taking this decision close off?
- What does taking this decision open up?
- What's the second-order consequence that matters most?
- What's the third-order consequence that might surprise you?
- How reversible is this decision?
- What's the lowest-cost reversal if it turns out wrong?
- Who else needs to be on board, and how does this decision land with them?
- What part of your life will be different the day after?
- What part of your life will be different a year after?
- What's the version of yourself that does this decision well — what's true about them?
- What's the version that doesn't — what's true about them?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_5_3_STRESS_TEST_QUESTIONS -->
- Imagine it's two years from now and this decision failed badly. Why?
- Imagine it succeeded but at a hidden cost. What was the cost?
- Is there a friend or trusted advisor who would push back on this — what would they say?
- Is the alignment between this decision and your stated criteria as strong as it looked, or is there a hidden gap?
- If the decision were taken from you tomorrow, would you be relieved or upset?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_5_3_REFRAMING_QUESTIONS -->
- What's the decision look like from the other person's perspective?
- What would Future-You wish Today-You knew before taking this step?
- What does this decision say about you, and is that something you're at peace with?
- If you were advising your sibling or close friend in this exact situation, what would you say?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_5_3_CLOSING_QUESTIONS -->
- Are you ready to make this decision, or does it need more time to settle?
- Is there a specific question that, if answered, would let you commit?
- Are you committing to the decision, or to making the decision (i.e., do you want to take a beat before acting)?
<!-- /SECTION -->
