# Phase 2.1 — Articulate Reasons to Resolve

For Step 2.1: surfacing why this matters and what is at stake.

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_2_1_OPENING_QUESTIONS -->
- Why does this matter to you?
- What happens if you don't resolve this?
- What's at stake?
- What do you stand to lose if this stays the way it is?
- What do you stand to gain by resolving it?
- Why now — what made today the day this surfaced?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_2_1_CLARIFYING_QUESTIONS -->
- What's the cost of inaction, in concrete terms?
- What's the cost of action, in concrete terms?
- Who else is affected by the outcome?
- What part of your life does this most touch — work, relationships, health, identity, money?
- Is this urgent, important, or both?
- What does this question get in the way of right now?
- What does this question reveal about what you value?
- What's the deepest reason you'd be willing to do hard work on this?
- What would change about how you spend your days if this were resolved?
- What would change about how you see yourself?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_2_1_STRESS_TEST_QUESTIONS -->
- Are the reasons you stated yours, or inherited from someone else (parent, partner, culture)?
- Would you still care about this six months from now without the current pressure?
- Is there a version of this where the right move is to not resolve it — to let it stay open?
- Is the reason you stated the actual reason, or the acceptable reason?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_2_1_REFRAMING_QUESTIONS -->
- What's the reason behind the reason?
- If the reason you stated isn't quite right, what would the truer reason be?
- What's the reason a 10-years-older version of you would give?
- What's the reason a 10-years-younger version of you would give?
- What's the reason that would surprise the people who know you best?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_2_1_CLOSING_QUESTIONS -->
- Have we landed on a set of reasons that feels like yours?
- Is the motivation here strong enough to carry through the work this will take?
<!-- /SECTION -->
