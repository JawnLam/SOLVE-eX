# Phase 6.3 — Execution Notes

For Step 6.3: surfacing execution-specific risks, constraints, and concrete operational details that bridge plan → action. The plan exists; this step pressure-tests it against the world it has to land in.

**See also:**
- `meta-questions/probing-questions.md` — when an execution note is generic and needs concrete specifics.
- `by-clarification-need/clarification-on-path.md` — when execution notes reveal confusion about *how* the plan will actually happen.
- `phase-6-2-action-plan.md` — back-reference when execution notes surface gaps in the plan itself.
- `phase-4-3-anticipative-solutions.md` — forward-reference when execution risks suggest the anticipative-solution work needs another pass.
- `phase-5-2-score-options.md` — back-reference when execution constraints reshape the evaluation criteria for option choice.

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_6_3_OPENING_QUESTIONS -->
- What's the first thing you'd do when you start executing — concretely?
- Walk me through what the first 48 hours of execution looks like.
- Who needs to know about this plan before it starts moving?
- What's the first thing that could go wrong when execution starts?
- What's the resource (time, money, attention, people) you'll be drawing on most heavily in the first week?
- What's the smallest unit of progress you can commit to delivering in the first week?
- What's a specific decision you'll need to make on day 2 that you haven't pre-decided yet?
- Who's the first person who'll observe whether the plan is actually being executed?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_6_3_CLARIFYING_QUESTIONS -->
- What part of the plan depends on someone else's cooperation that you don't yet have?
- What part of the plan depends on conditions you don't control?
- What's the cost of the first reversible step vs. the first irreversible step?
- When you commit to this plan, what else are you implicitly NOT doing — what's the opportunity cost?
- What's the timing constraint that, if missed, makes the plan harder or impossible?
- What's the communication that needs to happen with each stakeholder — and when?
- What's the artifact (document, message, scheduled meeting, draft) you'll produce in the first week as evidence the plan is moving?
- What's your day-1 to-do list, in concrete tasks?
- What constraints are operating that the plan assumes will hold — and how confident are you they will?
- Which step of the plan most depends on your own follow-through discipline?
- Which step most depends on someone else's follow-through discipline?
- What's the energy / attention budget the plan assumes you have available — and is that realistic this week?
- What's the most uncertain handoff in the plan — where one party's output becomes another's input?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_6_3_STRESS_TEST_QUESTIONS -->
- What's the assumption in the plan that, if false, derails the whole thing? (the load-bearing assumption)
- If the most cooperative stakeholder turns out to be uncooperative, what's plan B?
- If the timing constraint shifts by 50% in either direction, what changes?
- What's a contamination risk — a part of the plan that, if exposed prematurely, gets distorted?
- What's the first metric that would tell you "the plan is not working as expected"?
- Where in the plan are you most likely to procrastinate, avoid, or quietly drop?
- If you got hit by a bus tomorrow, what's the single most important thing about the plan that needs to be documented so others could continue?
- What's a foreseeable disruption (illness, market shift, personnel change, regulatory change) that would force a replan, and how would you handle it?
- What's the smallest concession (in scope, timeline, or quality) that would preserve the plan if a key resource gets cut?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_6_3_REFRAMING_QUESTIONS -->
- If you described the execution plan to someone who'd done this exact thing before, what would they immediately spot as missing?
- If a project manager from a different industry looked at this plan, what would they call out?
- What's the plan from the perspective of the person it's being done TO (not done BY)?
- What's the plan from the perspective of someone who wants it to fail?
- What's a piece of the plan that's currently implicit and should be written down?
- What's a piece of the plan that's currently written down but should actually be a verbal conversation?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: PHASE_6_3_CLOSING_QUESTIONS -->
- Are the execution notes specific enough that you could hand the plan to a deputy and have them act on it without ambiguity?
- What's the one execution detail that, if mishandled, derails the whole effort?
- Is there a pre-launch checklist that would be valuable, and do you have time to build it before execution starts?
- What's the post-execution review schedule — when will you check whether the plan worked as expected?
- When the plan inevitably surprises you, what's your decision-protocol for adjusting in flight?
- Who'll be your sanity-check partner during execution — someone outside the plan who'll notice if you're drifting?
<!-- /SECTION -->
