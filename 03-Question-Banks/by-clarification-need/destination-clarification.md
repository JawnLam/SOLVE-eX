# Destination-Clarification Questions

For use when the user's **target state** (Destination) is Unclear or
Partially-clear. Bank applies regardless of phase.

<!-- SECTION: DESTINATION_CLARIFICATION_OPENING_QUESTIONS -->
- If this worked out, what would be different?
- What would "good" look like here?
- Picture a future where this question is behind you. What's the scene?
- What were you hoping would change by working on this?
- If you could wave a wand and have one thing be different, what would it be?
- What's the outcome you'd find surprising and welcome?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: DESTINATION_CLARIFICATION_CLARIFYING_QUESTIONS -->
- When you say "X," what does that mean to you specifically?
- What's the difference between this outcome and a slightly better one — what's the upgrade?
- What's a version of the goal that's clearly too small?
- What's a version that's clearly too big?
- Who else benefits or suffers from this outcome being reached?
- What does this outcome let you do that you can't do now?
- What does this outcome stop, prevent, or release you from?
- If you wrote this goal on a piece of paper a year from now, what would you remember about today?
- What's a goal someone else (a friend, a parent, a partner) might have for you here that isn't yours?
- What's the goal you'd state if you weren't worried about looking foolish?
- What would you do tomorrow if you knew the goal was already reached?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: DESTINATION_CLARIFICATION_STRESS_TEST_QUESTIONS -->
- Six months from now, do you think you'd still want this same outcome, or would it have shifted?
- If someone you trust said "I think you want X instead of what you said," what would you say back?
- If you had to make this goal a single sentence with no qualifiers, what would it be — and is that sentence still right?
- What's the version of this you'd be embarrassed to admit you want?
- Is there a hidden assumption in this goal that, if false, would change everything?
- What's the difference between the goal you said and the goal you'd act on?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: DESTINATION_CLARIFICATION_REFRAMING_QUESTIONS -->
- What's the opposite of this outcome, and does naming the opposite clarify anything?
- If you couldn't have this outcome in this form, what would the closest acceptable alternative be?
- What's a different framing of the same desire — e.g., reaching toward versus moving away?
- Whose voice do you hear when you state this goal? Is it yours, or is it inherited?
- If we changed nothing else but the timeline (do this in 10 years instead of 1), how does the goal change?
- What's the goal that the goal you stated is actually pointing at?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: DESTINATION_CLARIFICATION_CLOSING_QUESTIONS -->
- Does this goal feel like yours, or like one you're stating because it seems reasonable?
- Are you ready to use this as the destination for the rest of this work?
- What would you want to add or sharpen before locking it in?
<!-- /SECTION -->
