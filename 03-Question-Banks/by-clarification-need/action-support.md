# Action-Support Questions

For use when the path is chosen and the user is implementing or following
up. Primarily for Phase 6 work but applicable any time action is the
question.

<!-- SECTION: ACTION_SUPPORT_OPENING_QUESTIONS -->
- What's the first concrete step?
- What's the time horizon for that step — today, this week, this month?
- Who needs to know about this first?
- What's the smallest version of the first step that counts as having started?
- What conditions need to be in place before you can take the first step?
- What's the cue that will trigger the next action — a date, an event, another person's move?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: ACTION_SUPPORT_CLARIFYING_QUESTIONS -->
- What's the resource you need that you don't yet have?
- Who's the accountability partner for this — or do you not need one?
- What's the deadline that's external versus self-imposed?
- What's the part of the plan that depends on something outside your control?
- What's a likely interruption to the plan, and what's your response if it happens?
- How will you know the action is working before the full outcome is reached?
- What would tell you to pause or revise the plan?
- What's the energy budget for this — how much capacity do you have for it given everything else?
- What's the part of the plan you keep avoiding when you write it down?
- What's the simplest version that still preserves the intent?
- What's already in motion that we can lean on?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: ACTION_SUPPORT_STRESS_TEST_QUESTIONS -->
- If you only did the first step and stopped, would that still be worth doing?
- What's the most likely way the plan fails in week one?
- What would a friend with strong follow-through say about this plan?
- Is the timing realistic given everything else on your plate, or are you assuming the rest of your life pauses?
- What's the assumption in this plan that, if wrong, makes the rest of it pointless?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: ACTION_SUPPORT_REFRAMING_QUESTIONS -->
- If you couldn't do this exact plan, what's the next-best version?
- What if the action was someone else's job? What would they need from you to make it work?
- What if you only had one hour a week to spend on this — what's the action that earns that hour?
- What would change if you treated this as a pilot rather than the final plan?
- What's the simplest test of the plan, before the full execution?
<!-- /SECTION -->

<!-- SECTION: ACTION_SUPPORT_CLOSING_QUESTIONS -->
- Does the plan as we have it feel doable when you sit with it for a moment?
- When and how will you check back on whether it's working?
- Is there anything you'd want to add or remove before treating this as the plan?
<!-- /SECTION -->
