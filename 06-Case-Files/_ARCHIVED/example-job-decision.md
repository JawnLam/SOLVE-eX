# Case File: Job decision

## Frame 0 — Job decision

### Frame opened: 2026-04-22T14:00:00

### Origin (clarity state: locked)

User has been at SmallCo for 3 years as a senior product manager. The
role has had stretches of high engagement but the last 6 months have
felt flat. Last week, an old colleague reached out with an offer for a
similar role at BigCorp — 30% comp bump, more strategic scope, more
travel, less autonomy.

### Destination (clarity state: locked)

A decision the user can stand behind — not because it's optimal on paper
but because it aligns with what the user actually values in work at this
life stage. The decision includes a clear "first 30 days" plan so the
transition (whichever way it goes) is executed cleanly.

### Reasons to Resolve (Step 2.1)

- Decision has a 2-week deadline from BigCorp.
- Existing flatness at SmallCo is producing anxiety regardless of offer.
- Partner is impacted (relocation possibility); user owes them a
  conversation that is grounded.

### Goal Statement (Step 2.2)

"Decide between BigCorp and SmallCo on the basis of fit-with-values
(scope, autonomy, proximity to family) — and not on comp alone — and
have a plan in motion by the deadline."

### Requirements (Step 2.3)

- Decision must be defensible to user's partner.
- Decision must be one user can hold against second-guessing for at
  least 6 months.
- Decision must not require user to misrepresent themselves to either
  employer.

### Current Facts (Step 3.1)

- BigCorp role: senior PM in strategy org, 30% comp uplift, 20%
  expected travel, reports to VP-1 (one removed from C-suite).
- SmallCo role: senior PM on growth team, 3 years tenure, current
  manager is a year into the role, no immediate promotion path
  surfaced.
- Partner is supportive but neutral; partner's job is location-locked
  in current city.
- User has been losing weekend hours to ruminating on work since Q1.

### Past Facts (Step 3.2)

- User has changed jobs every 3-4 years for the last 12 years; pattern
  is consistent.
- Last 3 transitions were driven by the same kind of agitation user is
  feeling now.
- Past transitions improved short-term satisfaction (3-6 months) but
  the underlying flatness pattern re-emerged in each.

### Systems Facts (Step 3.3)

- BigCorp recently announced restructuring; offer is for a team that
  is forming, not an established group.
- SmallCo is in a growth phase; user's team has expanded 2x in 18
  months; promotion path may surface in 6-9 months if user stays
  active.
- Industry overall is in flux around the user's specialty; both
  options are in roughly equally exposed positions.

### Future Facts (Step 3.4)

- 12-month projection at SmallCo: likely team lead role with some
  uplift; ongoing flatness uncertain.
- 12-month projection at BigCorp: scope expansion likely, but
  restructure risk is non-trivial.
- 3-year projection: at SmallCo, plateau risk; at BigCorp, more
  optionality but more volatility.

### Root Causes (Step 4.1)

- Surface cause: the offer arrived.
- Underlying cause: user's flatness at SmallCo has been building for
  6 months without being named or addressed.
- Deeper pattern: user's 3-4 year transition cycle is driven by the
  same un-addressed flatness pattern, not by genuine misfit with
  each job.

### Ideas (Step 4.2)

- A: Take BigCorp offer; accept comp + scope; manage relocation
  conversation with partner.
- B: Decline BigCorp; have a structured re-engagement conversation
  with SmallCo manager about scope and growth path.
- C: Decline BigCorp; have a deeper conversation with self about the
  pattern (i.e., the offer was the prompt, not the answer).
- D: Negotiate with BigCorp for remote/hybrid (no relocation); take
  if achievable; decline if not.
- E: Use the deadline to force a SmallCo conversation; decide based
  on SmallCo's response.

### Anticipative Solutions (Step 4.3)

- A's 12-month picture: financial uplift, scope expansion, but the
  flatness pattern may re-emerge by month 9.
- B's 12-month picture: same job, structured re-engagement conversation
  in place; success depends on manager's response.
- C's 12-month picture: deeper work on the pattern; possible decision
  to change careers, not jobs.
- D's 12-month picture: best-of-both if achievable; high-risk if
  negotiation fails.
- E's 12-month picture: SmallCo's response is the data; decision falls
  out of the data.

### Refined Options (Step 4.4)

Top three after refinement:

- **B/E hybrid:** Use the deadline to force a SmallCo conversation
  (E), with B as the path if SmallCo response is meaningful.
- **A:** Take BigCorp; accept partial relocation.
- **C:** Decline both as currently framed; take a 4-week sabbatical
  using vacation time to work on the underlying pattern; revisit.

### Evaluation Criteria (Step 5.1)

1. Defensibility to partner: how does each option feel after a
   30-minute partner conversation?
2. Multi-year alignment: does this option set up the next 3-5 years,
   or just the next 12 months?
3. Pattern-aware: does this option address the underlying flatness
   pattern or just relocate it?
4. Reversibility: how much does this close off?

### Decision Tool Used (Step 5.2)

Weighted Decision Matrix scored against the four criteria.

| Option | Defensibility | Multi-year | Pattern-aware | Reversibility | Total |
|--------|--------------|-----------|---------------|----------------|-------|
| B/E hybrid | 4 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 18 |
| A (BigCorp) | 3 | 3 | 2 | 2 | 10 |
| C (sabbatical) | 3 | 5 | 5 | 3 | 16 |

### Validation (Step 5.3)

Pre-Mortem on top option (B/E hybrid):

- Failure scenario: SmallCo manager responds with platitudes; user
  takes BigCorp; flatness re-emerges within 9 months.
- Cause: didn't define what "meaningful re-engagement" looks like
  before the conversation.
- Safeguard: Before the SmallCo conversation, user writes a list of
  what re-engagement would actually require — including scope, growth
  path, and pattern-recognition support (a coach or therapist).

Gut check: B/E hybrid felt right. C felt freeing but premature (the
pattern work can happen alongside the job, not in place of it).

### Design and Test (Step 6.1)

Test: have the SmallCo conversation in the next 5 days. Define ahead
of time what "meaningful response" looks like (per the validation
safeguard).

### Action Plan (Step 6.2)

1. (Day 1, within 24 hours) — Write the re-engagement criteria list.
2. (Day 2) — Have the SmallCo conversation; bring the list mentally.
3. (Day 3-4) — Process partner conversation about each scenario.
4. (Day 5-6) — Decision: based on SmallCo's response, either commit
   to B (decline BigCorp, agree to re-engagement plan with SmallCo) or
   commit to A (take BigCorp with full partner buy-in).
5. (Day 7) — Notify the non-chosen employer with grace.
6. (Week 2-4) — Begin therapy/coaching engagement to work the
   underlying pattern (this happens regardless of which job).
7. (Month 6) — Check-in: is the flatness pattern resolving?

### Execution Notes (Step 6.3)

(From session 2, 2026-04-25)
- SmallCo conversation went well — manager responded with concrete
  scope expansion proposal and committed to revisit growth path in
  Q3. User committed to B path.
- Partner conversation went well; partner expressed relief.
- BigCorp decline sent; relationship preserved.
- Coaching engagement scheduled for following week.

### Follow-up Review (Step 6.4)

(Recorded as a note at session 2 close; user will return in 30 days.)

- 7-day post-decision: user reports calmer than at any point in last
  6 months; the underlying pattern feels named and addressable.

### Resolution

Frame resolved 2026-04-25. User accepted SmallCo's re-engagement
proposal; declined BigCorp gracefully; scheduled coaching engagement
for pattern work. Case file marked resolved. User noted: "the decision
was less about which job and more about whether I was going to keep
re-running the same pattern."
